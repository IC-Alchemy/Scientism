# Experimental infection of chimpanzees with LAV (CDC MMWR 1984)

## Source type
- Role in this vault: historical surveillance document for the AIDS→HIV row; it summarizes two investigations and must be paired with their underlying primary papers
- Journal/year: *MMWR* 33(30):442–443, August 3, 1984
- Source basis in hand: full text from the CDC MMWR archive (web retrieval)

## What the document records
- **Investigation 1 (CDC/Yerkes/Institut Pasteur):** two previously negative chimpanzees received concentrated LAV with autologous in-vitro-infected lymphocytes, plus immunostimulation (diphtheria-tetanus toxoid, pneumococcal vaccine; one also received human lymphocytes). LAV was recovered from peripheral lymphocytes six days after inoculation — confirmed by reverse transcriptase assay, direct immunofluorescence, p25 radioimmunoprecipitation, and electron microscopy — and from six consecutive specimens over more than four months. Core-protein (p25) antibody appeared at three months. T4/T8 ratios trended downward in both animals, significantly below normal in only one. The report states: "No clinical illness has been detected in the animals, and physical examinations have remained normal."
- **Investigation 2 (NIH/Southwest Foundation):** two chimpanzees received plasma from a person with lymphadenopathy syndrome; transmission markers were seroconversion, depressed T4/T8 ratios, and severe prolonged lymphadenopathy in one animal coincident with seroconversion.
- **Editorial note:** prior primate transmission attempts (marmosets, rhesus, chimpanzees) with LAV, HTLV-III, or AIDS tissue had produced no infection beyond some lymphocyte changes; "opportunistic infections or tumors characteristic of AIDS have not developed" in these studies.

## What the document can answer
- That by August 1984 chimpanzee infection with LAV/HTLV-III was established via both virus inoculum and patient plasma, with serologic and virologic endpoints met and no AIDS-defining illness — the same infection-without-syndrome boundary as [[sources/alter-1984-htlv-iii-chimpanzees-plasma]].

## What it cannot answer
- Anything about long-term outcomes; it explicitly defers to ongoing follow-up.

## Later inferential-leap risk
The document is used in two opposite ways: as proof of transmission (the virologic endpoints are direct) and as proof that HIV does not cause disease (the "no clinical illness" line). The register keeps both bounded: infection endpoints were met under artificial high-dose exposure; the syndrome was not observed in this window, and the AIDS question was left to longer follow-up and the human epidemiology.

## Vault conclusion
Historical record: by August 1984, chimpanzees inoculated with LAV or patient plasma showed recoverable virus, seroconversion, and immune-ratio changes without clinical illness or AIDS-defining events — an infection-without-syndrome boundary at that date.
