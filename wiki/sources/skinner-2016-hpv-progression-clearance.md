# Progression of HPV infection to cervical lesions or clearance: VIVIANE control arm (Skinner et al. 2016)

## Source type
- Role in this vault: insufficiency/progression boundary paper; its control-arm design gives unvaccinated infection natural history inside a randomized trial framework
- Journal/year: *International Journal of Cancer*, 2016
- Source basis in hand: PubMed abstract only; full text not obtained (free at PMC4787275)

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Prospective analysis of the control (unvaccinated) arm of a phase III vaccine trial (VIVIANE, NCT00294047) in women over 25 |
| Subject / sample | 2,838 women in the control arm at 4-year interim analysis |
| Intervention or exposure | None within this analysis (observation of naturally acquired HPV infection); the trial context supplies standardized screening |
| Comparator / control | Oncogenic versus non-oncogenic types; 6-month persistent infection (6MPI) versus infection of any duration |
| Endpoint | Detection of CIN2+ associated with the same HPV type as the reference infection (Kaplan-Meier and multivariable Cox models); clearance of infections |
| Result | 1,073/2,838 (37.8%) had infections of any duration (2,615 total); 708 (24.9%) had 1,130 6MPIs. Six-month persistent oncogenic infection greatly increased CIN2+ risk: HPV33 HR 31.9 (8.3–122.2), HPV16 HR 21.1 (6.3–70.0), with HPV18, 31, 45 also significant. However, 79.3% of women with a baseline HPV infection cleared detectable infections of any duration, and 69.9% cleared a 6MPI. Progression risk in women over 25 resembled that of 15–25-year-olds in PATRICIA. Concomitant infection or CIN with a second oncogenic type raised risk further. |
| Main limitation | Abstract-level record; endpoint is CIN2+ detection under protocol screening, not cancer; short (4-year interim) follow-up; sponsor affiliation (GSK authors); persistence duration beyond 6 months not separately reported in the abstract. |

## What the abstract can answer
- Whether HPV infection alone equals lesion development: no — most infections cleared (79.3%), while risk concentrated in persistent oncogenic infections.
- Whether persistence rather than acquisition is the risk-carrying endpoint: yes, within this design.

## What full text would still need to establish
- Type-specific clearance curves; CIN3+ events; how often persistence beyond 6 months occurred and what followed it.

## Later inferential-leap risk
The same pair of leaps as the Manchester cohort: infection does not equal progression (most cleared), and high hazard ratios for persistent infection do not mean every persistent infection progressed — hazard ratios describe relative risk, and the abstract does not give absolute progression counts.

## Vault conclusion
Abstract-level: in an unvaccinated control arm with standardized screening, 79.3% of HPV infections cleared, and CIN2+ risk concentrated in 6-month persistent oncogenic infections (HPV16 HR 21.1) — a second direct source separating infection from progression.
