## What the source claims
- No mammalian speciation event has been experimentally reproduced in real time under controlled conditions.
- Human evolution and other deep-time evolutionary claims are said to rely on historical inference from fossils, genetics, and comparative patterns rather than direct replicated experiment.
- The note argues that this creates a distinction between observation of present-day patterns and interpretation of historical causation.
- It also suggests that paradigm commitments can shape how data are interpreted.
