# Long-term symptomless HIV-1 infection from a single donor (Learmont et al. 1992)

## Source type
- Role in this vault: progression-boundary paper for the AIDS→HIV row — a confirmed common-source transmission chain whose outcomes diverged sharply from other infected recipients
- Journal/year: *Lancet*, 1992
- Source basis in hand: PubMed abstract only; full text not obtained

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Prospective follow-up of a transfusion-linked HIV-1 cluster, compared with other transfusion-acquired infections |
| Subject / sample | Six recipients infected through blood products from a single common donor in New South Wales, Australia, plus the donor; comparison group of 101 other HIV-infected transfusion recipients with 7-year follow-up data |
| Intervention or exposure | None (observational); exposure was transfusion-acquired HIV-1 from one donor |
| Comparator / control | Progression frequency in the single-donor cluster versus the other transfusion recipients |
| Endpoint | Clinical symptoms, CD4 count, p24 antigenaemia, virus isolation and phenotype; progression to AIDS or CD4 < 0.50 × 10⁹/L |
| Result | Over follow-up of 6.8–10.1 years (donor 10.2 years), five of six recipients and the donor remained symptomless with normal CD4 counts and no p24 antigenaemia. HIV-1 was isolated from only one recipient — non-syncytium-inducing with limited in-vitro host range. The sixth recipient, extensively immunosuppressed for systemic lupus erythematosus, developed Pneumocystis pneumonia and died 4.3 years after infection. Progression frequency was 1/6 in the cluster versus 94/101 among other transfusion recipients (p < 0.0001). The authors attribute the course to a less virulent donor strain. |
| Main limitation | Abstract-level record; small cluster (n = 6); host factors (e.g., the immunosuppressed recipient) cannot be excluded from the abstract; virus was isolated from only one cluster member, so "less virulent strain" is an inference, not a demonstrated property for all six. |

## What the abstract can answer
- That a documented single-source HIV-1 transmission chain produced overwhelmingly nonprogressive infection over about a decade — infection with confirmed common source, without AIDS in five of six.
- That this outcome differs starkly and significantly from other transfusion-acquired infections in the same program (1/6 vs 94/101).

## What full text would still need to establish
- Viral sequence comparison across the cluster; HLA or host-factor analysis; updated follow-up.

## Later inferential-leap risk
This paper is sometimes quoted as "HIV doesn't cause AIDS" material. The vault's framing: it is a virulence/progression boundary inside a transmission chain that itself confirms transfusion-transmitted infection — the recipients were verifiably infected, and one did progress and die of an AIDS-defining illness. It bounds the uniformity and lethality of the causation claim, not the transmission fact.

## Vault conclusion
Abstract-level: a single-donor HIV-1 transmission cluster showed 1/6 progression to AIDS or CD4 decline over about a decade versus 94/101 in other transfusion recipients — long-term nonprogression on a confirmed common-source chain.
