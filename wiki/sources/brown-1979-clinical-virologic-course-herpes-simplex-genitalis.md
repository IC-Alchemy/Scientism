# Clinical and Virologic Course of Herpes Simplex Genitalis

## Source type
- Role in this vault: primary lesion-culture and clinical-course paper for genital herpes simplex
- Journal/year: *Western Journal of Medicine*, 1979
- Authors: Z. A. Brown and colleagues
- Source basis in hand: full-text PMC article

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Clinical virology course study |
| Subject / sample | Men and women with genital HSV |
| Exposure or intervention | Natural initial or recurrent genital herpes episodes |
| Comparator / control | Initial versus recurrent disease, male versus female, and wet versus dry lesions |
| Endpoint | Plaque-forming virus recovered from lesions, viral titer, and duration of excretion |
| Result | Initial female genital herpes had higher peak titers and longer excretion than recurrent disease; titers and excretion varied by disease status and lesion condition. |
| Main limitation | Older culture-based methods and heterogeneous lesions; does not measure modern PCR asymptomatic shedding frequency. |

## What the experiment can answer
- It can answer that cultivable HSV is recoverable from genital lesions and that titers/excretion vary by clinical context.

## What it cannot answer
- It cannot define all asymptomatic shedding or per-contact transmission probability.

## Later inferential-leap risk
The risk is treating lesion culture/titer as the whole transmission or recurrence story.

## Vault conclusion
Brown et al. 1979 supports a genital HSV lesion-culture and course rung, not complete transmission risk.
