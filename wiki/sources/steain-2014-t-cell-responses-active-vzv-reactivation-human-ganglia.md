# Analysis of T Cell Responses during Active Varicella-Zoster Virus Reactivation in Human Ganglia

## Source type
- Role in this vault: primary postmortem human ganglia paper for active VZV reactivation and immune response
- Journal/year: *Journal of Virology*, 2014
- Authors: M. Steain and colleagues
- Source basis in hand: full-text PMC article

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Postmortem human ganglia study during active herpes zoster |
| Subject / sample | Two zoster cases plus controls |
| Exposure or intervention | Natural active VZV reactivation |
| Comparator / control | Reactivated ganglia versus unaffected/control ganglia |
| Endpoint | VZV DNA, ganglionitis, T-cell infiltrates, and MHC changes |
| Result | Reactivated ganglia had VZV DNA, ganglionitis, T-cell infiltrates, and MHC changes. |
| Main limitation | Only two zoster cases; incidental/postmortem setting limits generalization. |

## What the experiment can answer
- It can answer that active zoster can involve inflammatory immune responses in affected human ganglia.

## What it cannot answer
- It cannot define general frequency, recurrence risk, or trigger.

## Later inferential-leap risk
The risk is generalizing a two-case postmortem finding into a complete shingles mechanism.

## Vault conclusion
Steain et al. 2014 supports an active VZV reactivation and ganglionic immune-response rung.
