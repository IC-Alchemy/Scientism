# Li 2014 (IMB/CAMS) - human diploid-cell inactivated EV71 vaccine, randomized placebo-controlled phase 3, HFMD endpoint

> [!note] Note
> Full text read and verified 2026-11-11 (loop run 23, queue item 44) from the complete 9-page publisher PDF (N Engl J Med 2014;370:829-837, "Downloaded from nejm.org on August 31, 2014") recovered from the 2014-08-31 Wayback capture of www.nejm.org/doi/pdf/10.1056/NEJMoa1303224 (application/pdf, 200, 360 KB); live nejm.org Cloudflare-walled, no PMCID. Citation confirmed against PubMed (PMID 24571755). Authors: Rongcheng Li, Longding Liu, Zhaojun Mo, ... Qihan Li (31 authors; Guangxi Provincial CDC, Yunnan Key Laboratory of Vaccine Research / Institute of Medical Biology of the Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences and Peking Union Medical College (Kunming), Fudan University, Fourth Military Medical University, National Institutes for Food and Drug Control). **Funded by state programs (National Basic Research Program 2011CB504903; National High-Tech R&D Program 2012AA02A404; State Project for Essential Drug Research and Development 2012ZX09101319) - not by a manufacturer grant line; the vaccine was developed by the Institute of Medical Biology.** Data collected by Guangxi CDC (EpiData double entry); analyzed by biostatisticians at Fourth Military Medical University and Fudan University; an independent blinded DSMB confirmed every diagnosis before unblinding. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01569581. Every number below is from the article itself.

## What experiment was run

- **Study type:** multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled phase 3 trial; seven counties in the Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region; March 2012 through February 2013.
- **Subjects:** 14,445 children assessed; **12,000 healthy children 6-71 months randomized 1:1 (6000/6000)**, stratified by age (6-23 vs 24-71 months) and randomized in blocks of eight; withdrawal 8.4%; **per-protocol 5481/5499** (two doses + completed 12-month follow-up); intention-to-treat = all randomized.
- **Intervention:** inactivated EV71 vaccine, genotype C4 strain isolated in the 2008 Fuyang pandemic, cultured in the **human diploid-cell line KMB17**, purified and inactivated; **100 U antigen adsorbed to 0.5 mg aluminum hydroxide**, 0.5 ml buffered saline, intramuscular, two doses 4 weeks apart.
- **Comparator (sham arm YES but NOT inert), quoted: "The same volume of aluminum hydroxide in buffered saline without antigen was used as a placebo."** Adjuvant-matched (alum present, antigen absent); vials carried identical computer-generated labels.
- **Surveillance:** a health-care-center-based surveillance system plus parental reporting; **village doctors contacted every participant once every 2 weeks** (home visit or telephone) to find suspected HFMD; suspected cases referred to county/city hospitals; throat swabs and stool screened by qRT-PCR at the Guangxi CDC central laboratory with confirmation by the National Institutes for Food and Drug Control; acute-phase (<=3 days) and convalescent (10-15 days) sera; the DSMB reviewed clinical and laboratory results and confirmed the diagnosis before unblinding.
- **Endpoint named exactly:** suspected HFMD = febrile illness (>37.5 C) with papular or vesicular rash in the characteristic distribution (oral mucosa, hands, feet, or buttocks); **primary endpoint = EV71-associated HFMD, defined as a suspected case with EV71 detected in a throat swab or stool specimen by quantitative RT-PCR**; severe EV71-HFMD = with neurologic, respiratory, or circulatory complications per Chinese Ministry of Health criteria. Efficacy window: **from 2 weeks after completion of the two-dose schedule until 1 year after the first dose** (~11 months, covering the May and October epidemic peaks). Secondary endpoints: severe HFMD in the same window; neutralizing-antibody seropositivity at days 56 and 180 in a prespecified immunogenicity subgroup (1,100 randomized; analyzed 401/402 after excluding pre-existing titers >=1:8, i.e. 57/65 infected before enrollment).
- **Power:** 5,600/group for 90% power at two-sided alpha 0.05, assuming 75% VE, 5 EV71-HFMD cases per 1,000 children/year (from 3-year Guangxi surveillance), 20% withdrawal.

## Result

- 594 suspected HFMD cases in the catchment area over the study (70.1% between May and July); 202 vaccine vs 392 placebo.
- **Primary endpoint (ITT): EV71-associated HFMD 4 vs 151 cases; vaccine efficacy 97.4% (95% CI 92.9-99.0, P<0.001)**; per-protocol 97.3% (92.6-99.0). Age strata: 6-23 months 2/3500 vs 94/3500 = 97.9% (91.4-99.5); 24-72 months 2/2500 vs 57/2500 = 96.5% (85.6-99.1).
- **Pathogen specificity - the trial's negative rows:** coxsackievirus A16 HFMD 48 vs 54, efficacy **11.1% (95% CI -30.8 to 39.6, P=0.55)**; other enteroviruses 106 vs 128, 17.2% (-6.0-35.8, P=0.15); clinically diagnosed HFMD regardless of cause 202 vs 392 = **48.5% (39.2-56.3)**. Sequencing: 87 of 155 EV71 genomes typed as C4 (the rest failed full-VP1 amplification).
- **Severe disease:** among the 155 EV71-HFMD cases, **2 severe cases, both in the placebo group; one of them died** (died within 24 h of hospital arrival, not counted as hospitalized).
- Single-dose subgroup: 0 cases among 514 single-dose vaccine recipients vs 2 among 498 single-dose placebo recipients - the authors call possible benefit but state statistical significance "was not reached, owing to the small number of single-dose recipients."
- **Immunogenicity:** seropositivity ITT **87.6% vs 4.1% at day 56** and 88.0% vs 26.4% at day 180; per-protocol **100% vs 3.5% at day 56** (GMT 170.6) and 99.3% vs 30.4% at day 180 (GMT 88.3); no age-stratum difference (P=0.92).
- **Safety:** fever within 7 days of an injection **41.6% vs 35.2% (P<0.001)**; any local event 5.9% vs 2.3% (P<0.001; swelling 1.8% vs 0.4%); grade-3+ fever 2.4% vs 2.5% (P=0.95); **SAEs 68 (1.1%) vs 125 (2.1%) (P<0.001)** - driven by HFMD hospitalization 41 vs 88; injection-related SAEs 2 vs 2; deaths 1 vs 1 (vaccine arm: traffic accident; placebo arm: severe EV71-HFMD); 28-day adverse events 47.4% vs 49.8% (P=0.009).

## Main limitations (authors' own)

- "the immunogenicity follow-up was stopped at 6 months, which was only the middle of the study period. The opportunity to understand the dynamics of neutralizing antibodies was therefore not addressed" (antibody-persistence monitoring continued separately in the phase 1 cohort and this subgroup).
- "because of limitations associated with the double-blind design, a detailed characterization of the immune response induced on virus challenge was not conducted among the vaccinated participants" (deferred to a small phase 2 trial).
- Boundaries recorded by this vault: baseline-serostatus exclusion means the immunogenicity per-protocol set is seronegative-enriched; efficacy excludes the first 2 weeks after dose 2 (incubating-infection window); the severe-case count (2 events) is too small for any severity claim beyond "both severe cases were placebo"; the trial measures no transmission, carriage, or all-cause outcome.

## Which gap and which ladder rung this fills

- **GAP 4 (intervention with disease endpoint), replication row for hand-foot-and-mouth disease / enterovirus 71** - second platform (human diploid-cell KMB17, 100 U vs Vero-cell 400 U), second province (Guangxi vs Jiangsu), different age range (6-71 vs 6-35 months), different case-finding structure (2-weekly village-doctor contact vs weekly study-staff visits), different funder (state programs vs Sinovac Biotech), same year, same directional result with a tighter point estimate (97.4% vs 94.8%), and the same two structured negatives (no CA16 effect; efficacy against all-cause HFMD bounded by EV71's share of cases). With [[sources/zhu-2014-sinovac-ev71-hfmd-rct]], the HFMD GAP 4 rung is platform-replicated like rotavirus and pertussis.
- The closed primaries of the third (Beijing Vigoo) EV71 trial - Zhu FC et al., Lancet 2013;381(9882):2024-2032, PMID 23726161, abstract-recorded VE 90.0% (67.1-96.9) against EV71-HFMD in 10,245 children with an alum-placebo - were not reached full-text this run (Unpaywall closed, no repository copy, no archived full text); the abstract is recorded here as a bibliographic lead only, not as verified evidence.

## Inferential leaps recorded

- "EV71, a major pathogen of hand, foot, and mouth disease, may be controlled with the use of a vaccine" (Conclusion): "controlled" is a population-level claim; the trial measured individual disease in 12,000 children in two counties for 11 months, not control of circulation - no carriage, transmission, or indirect-effect endpoint exists.
- The single-dose "may have benefited" sentence is drawn from an unrandomized, unpowered post hoc contrast (0 vs 2 cases).
- Severity/mortality inference from two placebo-only severe cases and one death - directionally consistent with the Sinovac trial's 0 vs 8 severe cases, but not independently meaningful at this sample size.
- "Fewer serious adverse events were observed among vaccine recipients" - numerically true (68 vs 125) but the imbalance is the disease-prevention effect itself (HFMD hospitalizations 41 vs 88), not a general safety signal; injection-related events were 2 vs 2.
