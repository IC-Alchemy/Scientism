# Asymptomatic Reactivation of Herpes Simplex Virus in Women after the First Episode of Genital Herpes

## Source type
- Role in this vault: primary cohort paper for asymptomatic genital HSV reactivation after first episode
- Journal/year: *Annals of Internal Medicine*, 1992
- Authors: D. M. Koelle and colleagues
- Source basis in hand: full-text PMC article

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Cohort follow-up after first-episode genital herpes |
| Subject / sample | 297 women with routine follow-up visits |
| Exposure or intervention | Natural post-first-episode course |
| Comparator / control | HSV-1 versus primary HSV-2 versus nonprimary HSV-2 groups |
| Endpoint | Asymptomatic genital HSV isolation by culture during routine visits |
| Result | Asymptomatic shedding was documented at 90 of 2401 routine visits; first-year detection occurred in 10% of genital HSV-1, 18% of primary HSV-2, and 23% of nonprimary HSV-2 groups. |
| Main limitation | Culture under-detects compared with later PCR methods. |

## What the experiment can answer
- It can answer that asymptomatic genital HSV reactivation occurs after first episodes and differs by viral type/status under culture detection.

## What it cannot answer
- It cannot define the full PCR-detectable shedding burden or per-act transmission.

## Later inferential-leap risk
The risk is treating culture-based visit positivity as total shedding burden.

## Vault conclusion
Koelle et al. 1992 supports asymptomatic genital HSV reactivation after first episode, with method limits.
