# Zhu 2014 (Sinovac) - Vero-cell inactivated EV71 vaccine, randomized placebo-controlled phase 3, HFMD/herpangina endpoint

> [!note] Note
> Full text read and verified 2026-11-11 (loop run 23, queue item 44) from the complete 11-page publisher PDF (N Engl J Med 2014;370:818-828, "Downloaded from nejm.org on August 31, 2014", stamped by the journal) recovered from the 2014-08-31 Wayback capture of www.nejm.org/doi/pdf/10.1056/NEJMoa1304923 (application/pdf, 200, 359 KB) - the same bronze-OA capture route as the run-15 Belshe 1998 ingest; live nejm.org is Cloudflare-walled per prior runs and no PMCID exists. Citation confirmed against PubMed (PMID 24571754). Authors: Fengcai Zhu, Wenbo Xu, Jielai Xia, Zhenglun Liang, ... Hua Wang, Nan Wang (31 authors; Jiangsu Provincial CDC, China CDC's National Institute for Viral Disease Control, National Institutes for Food and Drug Control, Sinovac Biotech, Fourth Military Medical University). **Sponsorship statement quoted: "The study sponsor [Sinovac Biotech] had no role in data collection, analysis, or interpretation or in manuscript preparation"**; analyses were run by statisticians at the Fourth Military Medical University; an independent five-member DSMB "made the final determination of the efficacy end points." Funded by Sinovac Biotech; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01507857. Every number below is from the article itself.

## What experiment was run

- **Study type:** multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled phase 3 trial; three counties (Ganyu, Taixing, Sheyang), 35 sites, Jiangsu Province, China; enrolled January 2012 before the HFMD seasonal peak.
- **Subjects:** 12,446 children assessed for eligibility; **10,077 healthy children 6-35 months** received at least one dose (safety cohort 5044 vaccine / 5033 placebo); 4719/4711 received dose 2 (93.6%); **ITT efficacy cohort 5041/5028** entering surveillance at day 57; per-protocol efficacy 4587/4578; 9,165 (90.9%) completed the 12-month surveillance per protocol. Arms balanced on age/sex/height/weight (Table 1).
- **Intervention:** Vero-cell-based inactivated human EV71 vaccine, seed strain H07 (subgenotype C4), **400 U antigen with alum (aluminum hydroxide) adjuvant**, 0.5 ml, intramuscular days 0 and 28.
- **Comparator (sham arm YES but NOT inert), quoted: "The EV71 vaccines contained 400 U of EV71 antigen with alum adjuvant, whereas the placebo contained aluminum hydroxide diluents with no EV71 antigen; both were packaged in syringes (0.5 ml per vial). Vaccine and placebo were supplied in coded, identical-appearing, single-dose vials."** The placebo is adjuvant-matched (aluminum hydroxide present, antigen absent) - reactogenicity comparisons are versus an alum injection, not a null injection.
- **Surveillance:** parents/guardians sought care at designated services (640 village clinics, 32 township hospitals, 7 county hospitals) PLUS study-staff home visits at least weekly; any illness (except specified unrelated conditions) screened; throat and anal swabs taken as soon as possible; quantitative real-time PCR discriminating EV71 from other enteroviruses; after a first EV71-RNA-positive test, serial swabs/stools at 3-day (acute) and 7-day (recovery) intervals plus acute-phase serum; all specimens to a central laboratory.
- **Endpoint named exactly (primary):** EV71-associated hand, foot, and mouth disease **or herpangina**, where virologically confirmed EV71-associated disease = "cases with two consecutive positive results for EV71 RNA on real-time PCR assay or positive results for EV71 on viral isolation and analysis of the VP1 sequence in stool samples or throat and anal swabs"; the DSMB reviewed all epidemiologic and laboratory data per case before unblinding and classified it as HFMD, herpangina, or other disease. Secondary endpoints: severe HFMD (neurologic or other serious complications), EV71-associated hospitalization, all EV71-associated diseases. Surveillance window: day 57 to month 14 (12 months).
- **Power:** 5,000/group for 80% power that the lower bound of the 95% CI for efficacy exceeded the placebo rate, assuming 80% VE and 8 EV71-HFMD/herpangina cases per 1,000 person-years in unvaccinated children.

## Result

- **Primary endpoint (1-year ITT):** EV71-associated HFMD or herpangina **5 vs 90 cases; efficacy 94.8% (95% CI 87.2-97.9)**; at 6 months 2 vs 80 = 97.5% (90.0-99.4). All EV71-associated disease 13 vs 106 = 88.0% (78.6-93.2). The 119 total EV71-associated cases broke down as 99 HFMD/herpangina (83.2%), 15 respiratory (12.6%), 3 gastrointestinal (2.5%), 2 febrile syndrome (1.7%).
- **Severe disease and hospitalization:** EV71-associated hospitalization **0 vs 24** (efficacy 100%, 95% CI 83.7-100); severe HFMD **0 vs 8** (all 8 with neurologic complications; 100%, CI 42.6-100; P=0.004).
- **Pathogen specificity - the trial's negative rows:** 1,950 HFMD/herpangina cases occurred in the surveillance period, of which 627 (32.2%) were caused by coxsackievirus A16; **efficacy against CA16 disease 6.6% (95% CI -9.2 to 20.1) - none - and against ALL HFMD/herpangina regardless of cause 19.8% (11.0-27.7)**. EV71 accounted for only 99 of 1,950 cases in this area-season.
- **Immunogenicity subgroup (1,291 recruited from four sites; day-0 blood only here):** baseline seropositivity 15.0% vaccine / 18.0% placebo (>=1:8); day-56 seroconversion 88.1% (85.2-90.6) per protocol, GMT 165.8 (145.9-188.5), >=1:16 in 97.1%; GMT declined to 88.8 at month 8 then levelled at 92.1 at month 14; all 11 EV71-HFMD/herpangina cases in this subgroup were placebo recipients seronegative at baseline. Exploratory 1:5 matched case-control analysis: titer 1:16 had maximal sensitivity+specificity - "may be considered as a possible serologic marker for protection" (the paper's hedge quoted).
- **Infected-vaccinees shedding:** among EV71-infected participants, mean shedding 5.0 days (vaccine) vs 7.9 days (placebo), not significant.
- **Safety:** SAEs 111 (2.2%) vs 131 (2.6%); injection-related SAEs 5 vs 6; deaths 2 vs 4 (5 drownings total + 1 traffic accident - none disease-related); solicited reactions within 7 days 51.4% vs 52.4% (fever 34.7% vs 35.1%; grade-3 reactions 3.0% vs 3.1%).

## Main limitations (authors' own)

- "no assessment of etiologic factors other than EV71 was performed in participants with EV71-associated disease, and we could therefore not exclude the possibility of coinfection with other pathogens."
- Baseline serostatus known only in the immunogenicity subgroup (15-18% seropositive); the authors assume even distribution by randomization.
- The authors' own observation that EV71 caused "only a small proportion" of HFMD/herpangina in the trial area-season (99/1,950) means the vaccine "did not significantly decrease the overall incidence of hand, foot, and mouth disease or herpangina."
- Duration paradox, quoted: "vaccine efficacy could be better observed in a population with high EV71 prevalence, but the duration of immunogenicity elicited by the vaccine could be affected by natural infections during EV71 epidemics. Therefore, data from this trial may lead to an overestimation of the duration of neutralizing antibodies."
- Cross-protection to other EV71 subgenotypes (B3/B4; vaccine is C4) has "not been tested yet in clinical trials" (animal immunogenicity data only); C4 dominance made strain-specific efficacy untestable within the trial.

## Which gap and which ladder rung this fills

- **GAP 4 (intervention with disease endpoint, not surrogate), first row for hand-foot-and-mouth disease / enterovirus 71.** The HFMD/enterovirus ladder previously stopped at lesion isolation, serology, EV71-outbreak/CNS association, and a tree-shrew model - no intervention rung. This trial randomizes a blocking intervention and counts virologically confirmed clinical HFMD/herpangina, hospitalization, and neurologic-severe HFMD; it also holds a within-trial pathogen-specificity negative (no CA16 effect) that separates an EV71-specific mechanism from a nonspecific one - stronger than the placebo-arm contrast alone.
- Together with [[sources/li-2014-imb-ev71-hfmd-rct]] (different manufacturer, cell substrate, dose, province, age range, and season), the GAP 4 rung for EV71 is replicated across two independent trials in the same year.

## Inferential leaps recorded

- The 1:16 titer correlate is read into the future ("is consistent with probable protection ... for 1 year") although the correlation analysis is exploratory, unmatched sensitivity/specificity varied little across 1:8-1:32, and the trial was not designed to validate a correlate.
- "by analogy with poliomyelitis, vaccination may offer the best option for disease control" (Introduction) - analogy, not measured transmission or population effect; the trial measured neither carriage reduction nor indirect protection, and indeed found no significant drop in all-cause HFMD in the trial population.
- The duration-stability claim (month 8-14 level-off) is made while acknowledging natural-infection boosting may inflate it.
