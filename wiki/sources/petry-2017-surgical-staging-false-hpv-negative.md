# Surgical staging identified false HPV-negative cases (Petry et al. 2017)

## Source type
- Role in this vault: diagnostic-misclassification boundary paper — the strongest of the HPV-negative set for showing that "HPV-negative cervical cancer" is often "not cervical cancer"
- Journal/year: *Papillomavirus Research*, 2017
- Source basis in hand: PubMed abstract only; full text not obtained (free at PMC5883200)

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Prospective collection of smears with surgical staging and HPV re-testing |
| Subject / sample | 371 patients with biopsy-proven diagnosis of cervical cancer |
| Intervention or exposure | HC2 testing for high-risk HPV; in HC2-negative cases, additional HPV genotyping of smears and paraffin-embedded tissue blocks, plus surgical staging |
| Comparator / control | Staging-confirmed primary cervical cancer versus misclassified non-cervical cases |
| Endpoint | HC2 result; confirmation of cervical primary by surgical staging; HPV type among negative cases |
| Result | HC2 was negative in 31/371 (8.8%). Surgical staging showed 21/31 (68%) of the HC2-negative cases were not cervical cancer. Among 350 staging-confirmed primary cervical cancers, 340/350 (97.2%) were HC2-positive. Non-high-risk types were found in five cases (HPV53, 70, and three HPV73), high-risk types in four more (two HPV16, two HPV18). The single remaining case — a primary undifferentiated carcinoma of the cervix — was HPV-DNA negative by all tests. |
| Main limitation | Abstract-level record; the design presumes biopsy and staging accuracy; a single fully HPV-negative case cannot be independently adjudicated from the abstract. |

## What the abstract can answer
- That in this series the largest source of "HPV-negative cervical cancer" was misclassification (two-thirds of negative cases were not cervical cancer), followed by non-high-risk types, then assay failure.
- That under staging-verified primary cervical cancer, only one of 350 cases lacked detectable HPV DNA by all tests used.

## What full text would still need to establish
- Staging protocols and pathology review; which genotyping assays were used on blocks; clinical characteristics of the single negative case.

## Later inferential-leap risk
The vault-relevant warning: retrospective HPV-negative fractions computed without staging verification overestimate true HPV-negative cervical cancer (the authors' own conclusion). Conversely, the single verified negative case is one case — it cannot carry a "many cervical cancers are HPV-independent" claim.

## Vault conclusion
Abstract-level: with surgical staging, most HPV-negative "cervical cancers" in this series were misclassified primaries, leaving one HPV-DNA-negative primary cervical cancer among 350 (0.3%) under the tests used.
