# AIDS

## TL;DR
This raw file is a briefing note about Peter Duesberg's book *Inventing the AIDS Virus*. It is a research lead on HIV/AIDS causation disputes and AZT-toxicity arguments, not a source that can settle those disputes by itself.

> [!warning] Warning
> This page is for research analysis only. It is not medical advice.

## Source triage
- Type: commentary on a controversial book
- Signal: moderate as a map of dissenting claims
- Limitation: the raw note summarizes arguments but does not present a discrete experiment or a stable bibliography for verification

## What the source claims
- HIV is not the sole cause of AIDS.
- Long-term recreational drug use and AZT toxicity are presented as primary drivers of immune deficiency and AIDS-defining illness.
- Epidemiologic differences across regions are presented as evidence against a simple HIV-only explanation.

## What question the underlying evidence is capable of answering
- Toxicology studies can address whether specific drugs damage immune function.
- Epidemiologic comparisons can test associations and pattern consistency.
- Those lines of evidence do not, by themselves, resolve the full causal question unless the underlying studies are reviewed directly and the competing explanations are compared carefully.

## What this wiki currently concludes
- This raw file is useful as a lead on causation, toxicology, and inferential disputes in AIDS history.
- The note does not justify concluding that HIV is not causal, or that drug toxicity alone explains AIDS.
- Any strong conclusion here requires direct engagement with the cited book and higher-grade primary or review literature.
