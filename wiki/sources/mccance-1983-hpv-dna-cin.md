# Presence of HPV DNA sequences in cervical intraepithelial neoplasia (McCance et al. 1983)

## Source type
- Role in this vault: early partial-detection boundary paper — HPV6 (a low-risk type) present in a majority but not all CIN cases
- Journal/year: *British Medical Journal*, 1983
- Source basis in hand: PubMed abstract only; full text not obtained (free at PMC1549159)

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Cross-sectional clinic-based detection study |
| Subject / sample | 22 patients referred to a district colposcopy clinic for abnormal cytology or suspicious cervix, all found to have cervical epithelial abnormality |
| Intervention or exposure | Cytology, histology, immunohistochemistry (indirect immunoalkaline phosphatase), and DNA-DNA hybridisation for papillomavirus |
| Comparator / control | Concordance between cytologic, colposcopic, and histologic indicators of wart-virus infection; no disease-free control arm reported in the abstract (authors state control study was to follow) |
| Endpoint | HPV antigen detection; HPV type 6 DNA detection by histologic grade |
| Result | HPV antigen was found in 6/22 biopsy specimens; HPV6 DNA in 13/22 women, including 4 of 6 whose histology was CIN3. Where cytology, colposcopy, and histology all indicated wart-virus infection (8 cases), HPV6 DNA was found in 7. HPV6 DNA appeared in more than half of proved CIN cases. |
| Main limitation | Abstract-level record; the assay targets HPV6, not the later-identified high-risk types 16/18 (this predates their 1983-84 cloning in this vault's ladder); small n; no disease-free controls in this report. |

## What the abstract can answer
- That in 1983, in this clinic series, viral markers were present in most but not all CIN cases, and the type captured (HPV6) was not the type later central to the cancer association — a partial-detection and type-specificity boundary for the early literature.

## What full text would still need to establish
- Hybridisation conditions and probe validation; grade-by-grade detection table; the planned control-arm results.

## Later inferential-leap risk
This paper predates HPV16/18 discovery in cervical material ([[sources/durst-gissmann-ikenberg-zur-hausen-1983-hpv16-cervical-carcinoma]] is the same year). Its HPV6-heavy findings cannot be cited either for or against high-risk-HPV necessity; the boundary is that early detection was type- and method-limited.

## Vault conclusion
Abstract-level: in 22 women with cervical epithelial abnormality, HPV6 DNA was found in 13, including 4 of 6 CIN3 cases — early detection was partial and type-limited, which is a boundary condition on reading the early HPV-CIN literature, not evidence for or against the later HPV16/18 association.
