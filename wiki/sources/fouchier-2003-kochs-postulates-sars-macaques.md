# Aetiology: Koch's postulates fulfilled for SARS virus (Fouchier et al. 2003)

## Source type
- Role in this vault: challenge-rung record for [[synthesis/sars-sars-cov-evidence-ladder]] — one of the three named acquisition leads, now at abstract level; a short Nature correspondence whose experimental detail lives in supplementary material and in the group's companion paper
- Journal/year: *Nature*, 2003 (correspondence)
- Source basis in hand: PubMed abstract (with supplementary-material note); full text not extracted in this pass

## What the abstract can answer
- That the Erasmus group reported experimental macaque infection reproducing disease in support of SARS-CoV etiology, at this date and in this form — a challenge-rung claim requiring the companion full-text reconstruction for its boundaries.

## What full text (or the companion paper) would still need to establish
- Animal numbers, exposure route(s), control animals, pathology scoring, and re-isolation — the specific endpoint fields the vault requires.

## Later inferential-leap risk
"Koch's postulates fulfilled" is a rhetorical endpoint, not an experimental field. The vault's Kuiken-based reconstruction already records the actual challenge design's limits; this correspondence must be cited through those boundaries, not as independent closure.

## Vault conclusion
Abstract-level: the Erasmus group's Nature correspondence asserts disease reproduction in cynomolgus macaques experimentally infected with SARS-CoV; endpoint detail is not extractable here and the rung remains bounded by the Kuiken full-text reconstruction.
