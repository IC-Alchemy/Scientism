# A novel coronavirus associated with SARS (Ksiazek et al. 2003)

## Source type
- Role in this vault: isolation/characterization rung for [[synthesis/sars-sars-cov-evidence-ladder]] — one of the three named acquisition leads, now at abstract level
- Journal/year: *New England Journal of Medicine*, 2003
- Source basis in hand: PubMed abstract; full text not obtained

## Evidence fields
| Field | Extraction |
|---|---|
| Study type | Multicountry specimen-based pathogen search with isolation and molecular characterization |
| Subject / sample | Clinical specimens from patients meeting the SARS case definition, received from seven countries |
| Intervention or exposure | None — diagnostic and isolation workup (virus isolation, EM, histology, molecular and serologic assays across a wide pathogen panel) |
| Comparator / control | Systematic testing for previously described respiratory pathogens (none consistently identified); immunologic reactivity panel |
| Endpoint | Pathogen isolation; cytopathology in Vero E6 cells; EM ultrastructure; immunohistochemical/immunofluorescence reactivity; polymerase-gene RT-PCR sequence; patient RT-PCR positivity; serologic response |
| Result | No previously described respiratory pathogen was consistently identified. A novel coronavirus was isolated from SARS-case patients, with cytopathic effect in Vero E6 cells, coronavirus-characteristic ultrastructure, reactivity with group I coronavirus polyclonal antibodies, and a polymerase-gene sequence identifying a unique coronavirus only distantly related to known ones. Specific RT-PCR primers found identical sequences in 12 patients from several locations, consistent with a point-source outbreak, and the isolate supported virus-specific serologic assays. The authors concluded the novel coronavirus is associated with the outbreak with evidence of an etiologic role, naming the Urbani strain. |
| Main limitation | Abstract-level record; association plus characterization in the abstract; the etiologic-role claim rests on the full paper's (and companion papers') animal and serologic data; specimen-selection details not visible. |

## What the abstract can answer
- That a novel coronavirus was isolated from SARS patients, characterized by culture, EM, immunology, and sequence, with identical sequences across geographically separated patients — the isolation/characterization rung.

## What full text would still need to establish
- Per-specimen isolation rates, seroconversion timing, and the control panels behind "no other pathogen consistently identified."

## Later inferential-leap risk
The etiologic-role sentence is author synthesis; the ladder keeps isolation separate from the macaque challenge rung ([[sources/kuiken-2003-newly-discovered-coronavirus-primary-cause-sars]]) and from ordinary human transmission.

## Vault conclusion
Abstract-level: a novel coronavirus was isolated from SARS patients across seven countries, characterized by Vero E6 culture, EM, group I reactivity, and unique polymerase sequence, with identical RT-PCR sequences in 12 patients and virus-specific serology available.
