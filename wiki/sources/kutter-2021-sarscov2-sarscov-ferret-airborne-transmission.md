# Kutter et al. 2021 - SARS-CoV and SARS-CoV-2 transmitted through the air between ferrets over more than one meter distance

> [!note] Note
> Full text read and verified 2026-11-10 (loop run 18, queue item 33) from the complete Europe PMC full-text XML (PMC7955093, CC-BY). Every number below is from that full text. Authors: Jasmin S. Kutter, Dennis de Meulder, Theo M. Bestebroer, Pascal Lexmond, Ard Mulders, Mathilde Richard, Ron A. M. Fouchier, Sander Herfst (Department of Viroscience, Erasmus University Medical Center, Rotterdam).

## What experiment was run

- **Study type:** experimental airborne-transmission study with physical route separation and a positive-control virus; donors and recipients never share a cage; conducted in class III isolators in a BSL-3+ laboratory (Dutch project license 248 AVD1010020174312; Erasmus MC permits 17-4312-03/05/06).
- **Subjects:** influenza-, SARS-CoV-2-, and Aleutian-disease-virus-seronegative 6-month-old female ferrets (*Mustela putorius furo*), 640-1215 g, from a commercial breeder; four independently housed donor-recipient transmission pairs per virus (n = 4 per group).
- **Set-up (the exposure mechanism):** donor cage below, indirect-recipient cage above, connected by a hard duct of 15-cm-wide PVC pipes with four 90-degree turns; average air path 118 cm (range 73-163 cm); upward airflow donor-to-recipient at 100 L/min; steel grids over inlet and outlet with the bottom 5 cm closed, "to prevent spill-over of food, feces, and other large particles." Recipients were added one day after donor inoculation and were exposed only to air that had traveled through the duct.
- **Exposure:** donors inoculated intranasally (250 µL dropwise per nostril, under ketamine/medetomidine anesthesia): A/H1N1 A/Netherlands/602/2009 at 10^6 TCID50; SARS-CoV-2 isolate BetaCoV/Munich/BavPat1/2020 (provided by C. Drosten) at 6 x 10^5 TCID50; SARS-CoV isolate HKU39849 (provided by M. Peiris) at 1.6 x 10^6 TCID50.
- **Comparator/control:** sham arm NO - no mock-inoculated donor-recipient pairs exist in this paper (recorded explicitly). The control is a **positive-control virus**: pandemic A/H1N1 influenza, known to transmit through the air between ferrets over 10 cm, run through the same >1 m set-up first to validate it (4/4 recipients infected). Distance context is compiled in Table 1 from prior experiments: SARS-CoV-2 direct contact 4/4, 10 cm 3/4, >1 m 2/4; SARS-CoV >1 m 4/4 (direct contact 2/2 in a different set-up, intratracheal inoculation).

## Endpoints (named exactly)

- **Viral RNA shedding:** throat and nasal swabs from donors and recipients on alternating days (to prevent cross-contamination), qRT-PCR. Collection windows: A/H1N1 donors to 7 dpi / recipients to 13 dpe; SARS-CoV-2 both to 15 dpi/dpe; SARS-CoV donors to 9 dpi / recipients to 11 dpe (humane early euthanasia).
- **Infectious virus:** titration of the same swabs in VeroE6 cells (TCID50; TCID50-equivalent for SARS-CoV from a standard curve), defining productive infection.
- **Serology (confirms transmission events):** hemagglutination-inhibition assay for A/H1N1; nucleoprotein ELISA for SARS-CoV-2 and SARS-CoV.
- **No disease endpoint:** SARS-CoV-2 donors and recipients "did not display any symptoms of the disease"; predefined humane endpoints (anorexia, >20% weight loss, breathing difficulties, motor/behavioral change) triggered SARS-CoV animal euthanasia.

## Results

- **Set-up validation (A/H1N1, >1 m):** 4/4 recipient pairs transmitted, shedding onset 1-5 dpe; 3/4 recipients productively infected (titers 10^1.5-10^6.0 TCID50/mL, peaks and durations similar to donors); 3/4 seroconverted (HI), the RNA-positive/no-virus recipient not.
- **SARS-CoV-2 (>1 m):** 2/4 recipients infected - viral RNA from 3 dpe, peak levels 7 dpe (1.6 and 3.7 log10 TCID50-equivalents/mL), throat and nose RNA still positive at 15 dpe (last collection day); infectious virus isolated from every RNA-positive animal for at least two consecutive days; both seroconverted (NP ELISA) at 17 dpe; the two negative recipients did not seroconvert. Recipients showed no signs of illness, like the donors.
- **SARS-CoV (>1 m):** 4/4 recipients infected, onset 1-3 dpe, RNA peaks 3-5 dpe (4.0/3.6/3.4/2.6 log10 TCID50-equiv/mL); recipients euthanized at 11 dpe while still RNA-positive (breathing difficulties, reduced activity); all seroconverted. Donors euthanized at 9 dpi for the same signs. "The kinetics of virus shedding was similar in all animals, both in terms of duration and virus RNA levels" - despite donors receiving a large inoculation dose by a different route than the recipients' airborne exposure.
- **Fomite investigation (SARS-CoV fur):** SARS-CoV RNA detected in flank-fur swabs of all four donors (on average 240-fold / 7.9 Ct lower than throat/nose swabs; attributed to grooming), but **no infectious virus was isolated from any fur sample**. An aerodynamic particle sizer showed particles >10 µm present at both the donor outlet and the recipient inlet, i.e., the high flow rate can carry larger particles across the 1 m.
- **Sequencing:** spike substitutions N501T and S686G present in all donors and both SARS-CoV-2 recipients (>99% of reads), but already in the donor animals - "likely... selected due to adaptation to the new host, rather than having substantial importance for transmission."

## Authors' own boundaries (quoted)

- "Although the experiments do not discriminate between transmission via small aerosols, large droplets and fomites, these results demonstrate that SARS-CoV and SARS-CoV-2 can remain infectious while traveling through the air."
- "Although the evidence for virus transmission via the air between humans under natural conditions is absent or weak for SARS-CoV and SARS-CoV-2, ferrets may represent a sensitive model to study interventions aimed at preventing virus transmission."

## Main limitations

- n = 4 transmission pairs per virus; a 2/4 result has very wide uncertainty.
- No disease endpoint: the SARS-CoV-2 ferret model is asymptomatic, so this rung cannot support illness claims (same boundary as [[sources/lowen-2007-guinea-pig-humidity-temperature-transmission]]).
- Route non-discrimination (small aerosols vs large droplets vs aerosolized fomites) is stated by the authors themselves; the >10 µm particles at the recipient inlet mean the set-up demonstrates "infectious while traveling through the air" without specifying the physical carrier.
- SARS-CoV animals were euthanized early (9 dpi donors, 11 dpe recipients), truncating follow-up; SARS-CoV donors were inoculated in a separate earlier set-up for the direct-contact row (different inoculation route).
- No sham-inoculated animals; the null reference is implicit (seronegative recipients tested before exposure) rather than a randomized control group.

## Inferential leaps made by the authors (recorded, not endorsed)

- "Efficient virus transmission between ferrets is in agreement with frequent SARS-CoV-2 outbreaks in mink farms" - an ecological-compatibility inference, not an experiment on minks.
- The mink- and human-relevance framing of a ferret model ("ferrets may represent a sensitive model...") beyond what ferret-to-ferret duct transmission measures.
- The closing statement that the set-up "mimics" human transmission (Discussion) overstates what a 100 L/min forced-flow duct proves about natural human exposure conditions.

## What this experiment can and cannot answer

- **Can answer:** whether SARS-CoV-2 and SARS-CoV shed by inoculated ferrets can cross ~1.2 m of ducted air with grids blocking large spill-over and remain infectious enough to productively infect a fresh recipient (yes: 2/4 and 4/4, serology-confirmed, infectious virus recovered from every infected recipient), with a same-set-up A/H1N1 positive control at 4/4.
- **Cannot answer:** which physical carrier (aerosol vs droplet vs fomite) moved the virus; illness (no disease endpoint); human natural-route transmission; infectious dose received by recipients; transmission rates under natural airflow.

## Which gap and ladder rung this fills

- **GAP 2 (controlled natural-route transmission) - animal replication row for a second pathogen.** The corpus's animal rung held only [[sources/lowen-2007-guinea-pig-humidity-temperature-transmission]] (influenza guinea pigs, cage-separated wire-grid design). This paper replicates that design logic (physically separated recipients, exposure only through air, positive-control virus, serology-confirmed transmission) for SARS-CoV-2 and SARS-CoV, and extends the separation distance beyond 1 m. Recorded on the cross-cutting ladder in [[synthesis/viral-disease-causation-evidence-ladder]] and on [[synthesis/sars-cov-2-covid-causation-audit]] as the animal natural-route row.
- Complements the human GAP 2 rows (Cowling 2009, Aiello 2012, Suess 2012, Stebbins 2011 - all null on primaries) and the human GAP 3 household cohorts (Kelly 2022, Kleynhans 2023) with a controlled exposure mechanism no human study can provide.
