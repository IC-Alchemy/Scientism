# Pasteur's Researches on Rabies

## TL;DR
The raw vault file is a stub that points to an 1886 BMJ report on Pasteur's work on rabies and preventive inoculation. It is relevant because it may preserve near-contemporary reporting on the treatment claims, but the current raw note does not yet provide enough detail for strong conclusions.

> Note: This page is intentionally conservative because the raw note contains only bibliographic scaffolding and images, not the report text itself.

## Source type and scope
- Near-contemporary secondary report.
- Likely summarizes Pasteur's treatment claims and reported outcomes as understood in 1886.
- Not the same thing as Pasteur's full laboratory notebooks or a modern historical analysis.

## What the source is likely addressing
- Pasteur's method of preventive inoculation after suspected rabid-animal exposure.
- The reported treatment sequence and rationale.
- Contemporary evaluation of early cases and results.

## What question the report is capable of answering
- A contemporary medical report can help show how Pasteur's method was publicly described and clinically framed at the time.
- It can potentially document claimed endpoints such as survival after exposure, treatment schedule, and case selection.
- It cannot, by itself, settle modern disputes over rabies etiology, mechanism, or the total historical reliability of later Pasteur narratives.

## What this wiki currently concludes
- This source should be paired with both primary Pasteur material and later critical histories.
- At present it functions as an evidentiary placeholder showing that the rabies episode needs direct source work, not just later commentary.
- Stronger conclusions about efficacy or misconduct should not be filed back from this stub alone.
