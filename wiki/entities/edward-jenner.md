# Edward Jenner

## Why this page matters
Jenner matters here because later medical history often treats his work as a clean foundational success. The raw sources in this vault suggest a need to separate his reported observations, his broader explanatory theory, and later retrospective claims.

## What the sources say
- [[sources/edward-jenners-smallpox-paper-1798]] presents a critical reading of Jenner's 1798 inquiry and highlights both positive challenge results and notable ambiguities.
- [[sources/diagnosis-smallpox-viroliegy]] adds a broader caution that historical rash diagnosis could be unstable, which matters when reading old smallpox case narratives.

## What this wiki currently concludes
- Jenner's reported inoculation cases are historically central and should be read directly where possible.
- The current vault can only preserve a narrow reported-case lead: Jenner's account describes cowpox-material inoculation, later variolous challenge, and an observed non-smallpox response in at least some cases.
- The limitation is significant: the current wiki source is Tier 3 commentary around excerpts, not a direct full review of Jenner's 1798 text or later historical analysis.
- We do not yet know, from this first-pass ingest alone, how a balanced historical review would weigh the full record.

## Related source pages in this vault

These are the experimental and commentary pages behind the smallpox and vaccination claims Jenner is historically tied to.

### Experimental record
- [[synthesis/smallpox-variola-virus-evidence-ladder]] - vault synthesis page, not itself a source, mapping the Tier 1 experimental record for smallpox and variola: animal inoculation, route boundaries, egg propagation, monkey transmission, and reservoir limits.
- [[sources/brinckerhoff-tyzzer-1906-experimental-variola-monkeys]] - 1906 Tier 1 paper recording that human variola material inoculated into monkeys by skin, corneal, mucosal, and tracheal routes produced local lesions and sometimes generalized exanthem, with route-dependent results; the ladder files it as the artificial-inoculation and route-boundary rung.
- [[sources/nelson-1940-variola-vaccinia-survival-mouse-lungs]] - 1940 Tier 1 paper recording that prior variola exposure altered lung virus-recovery kinetics in mice and gave only partial cross-protection against later vaccinia challenge.
- [[sources/nelson-1943-stability-variola-virus-embryonated-eggs]] - 1943 Tier 1 paper recording that variola strains propagated in embryonated eggs remained behaviorally distinct from vaccinia across passage.
- [[sources/oya-1956-vaccinia-variola-one-day-hen-egg-growth]] - 1956 Tier 1 paper recording comparative growth of variola and vaccinia in one-day fertile hen eggs; a laboratory-growth and host-system endpoint only.
- [[sources/noble-rich-1969-smallpox-contact-aerosol-macaca-irus]] - 1969 Tier 1 paper recording that experimentally infected *Macaca irus* transmitted variola to cage-mates by contact and to separated downwind monkeys in an aerosol-chamber arrangement, within a model-bound monkey syndrome.

### Commentary and critique
- [[sources/edward-jenners-smallpox-paper-1798]] - Tier 3 ViroLIEgy commentary built around excerpts from the 1798 *Inquiry*; it argues the case record is more ambiguous than later retellings imply. It is commentary on the paper, not the paper itself.
- [[sources/diagnosis-smallpox-viroliegy]] - Tier 3 commentary arguing that historical smallpox rash diagnosis was unstable; its page records that its broader claim that several named diseases are one process is not established.

## Open questions
- The vault holds no Tier 1 copy of Jenner's 1798 inquiry itself; the only ingested material is Tier 3 commentary built around excerpts, so Jenner's reported cases cannot be treated as experimentally supported here until the primary text is ingested.
- No page in this vault records a human cowpox-protection endpoint. The smallpox ladder's Tier 1 rungs are animal-inoculation, egg-propagation, and monkey-transmission evidence, and per that ladder ordinary human smallpox transmission and purified-virus human challenge remain rungs not reconstructed in the current pass.
