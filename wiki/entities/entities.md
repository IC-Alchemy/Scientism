# Entities

People and outlets that recur across the source corpus. Historical figures are treated as sites of disputed interpretation; commentary outlets are dispute-mapping material, not evidence.

## Historical figures

- [[entities/edward-jenner|Edward Jenner]] — Historical physician associated with early smallpox vaccination claims, whose published case record in this vault is treated as important but not self-interpreting.
- [[entities/louis-pasteur|Louis Pasteur]] — Historical scientist whose work in this vault is treated as a disputed case study in the gap between experiment, treatment claims, and later historical narrative.
- [[entities/robert-koch|Robert Koch]] — Historical scientist whose laboratory methods and later reputation are central to this vault's questions about causal-proof standards in germ-theory history.
- [[entities/rene-dubos|Rene Dubos]] — Microbiologist referenced in the seed source as a lead for later criticism or qualification of germ-theory thinking.
- [[entities/paul-feyerabend|Paul Feyerabend]] — Philosopher of science referenced in the seed source as a lead for critiques of fixed scientific method and authority.

## Contemporary commentary outlets

- [[entities/mike-stone|Mike Stone]] — Contemporary revisionist commentator whose essays in this vault argue that canonical germ-theory history overstates what historical experiments proved.
- [[entities/viroliegy|ViroLIEgy]] — A commentary source used repeatedly in this vault for revisionist critiques of virology, smallpox history, Pasteur, and infectious-disease claims.
