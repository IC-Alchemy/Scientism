# Louis Pasteur

## What it is
[[entities/louis-pasteur]] is a central historical figure in microbiology and medicine. In this vault he is being tracked specifically through disputed rabies-history materials and through questions about how later prestige narratives relate to the underlying experiments and treatment claims.

## Why it matters
This page matters because Pasteur is a concrete historical case where the vault can test [[concepts/inferential-leap]] against named experiments, named endpoints, and competing historical interpretations.
The current vault has not yet ingested direct Pasteur fermentation sources, so fermentation claims should not carry evidentiary weight here.

## What the sources say
- [[sources/pasteurs-researches-on-rabies]] points to a near-contemporary BMJ report on Pasteur's rabies treatment program, but the current raw note is only a stub and still needs direct extraction.
- [[sources/louis-pasteurs-unethical-rabies-fraud]] argues that Pasteur's rabies work involved ethical and evidentiary problems, but it is a Tier 3 polemical source and cannot settle that claim on its own.
- [[sources/from-pasteur-to-panic-fear-fraud-and-the-fight-for-historical-truth]] places Pasteur inside a broader revisionist narrative about deception in medical history.
- [[sources/the-germ-hypothesis-part-2]] uses Pasteur mainly as background contrast while shifting focus to Koch's role in later germ-theory proof claims.

## What this wiki currently concludes
- Pasteur remains an unresolved case in this vault.
- Current Pasteur-related pages mostly concern rabies-history dispute leads, and the rabies source base is still too thin for direct experiment-level conclusions.
- Any future Pasteur claim should name the experiment, subject or material, comparator, endpoint, result, and limitation before it is used in synthesis.

## Related source pages in this vault

These are the experimental and commentary pages behind the rabies-history dispute tracked on this page.

### Experimental record
- [[synthesis/rabies-rabies-virus-evidence-ladder]] - vault synthesis page, not itself a source, mapping the Tier 1 experimental record for rabies: artificial inoculation, neural-spread pathology, chick-embryo propagation, particle morphology, tissue-culture replication, street/fixed strain differences, and route boundaries. The ladder records that it does not reconstruct ordinary bite transmission, human clinical rabies, or post-exposure prophylaxis evidence.
- [[sources/goodpasture-1925-rabies-neural-transmission-rabbits-negri-bodies]] - 1925 Tier 1 paper recording that street-rabies brain material injected into rabbit masseter muscle produced clinical rabies signs and lesion distributions compatible with neural spread; an artificial-route animal model, not ordinary bite transmission.
- [[sources/dawson-1941-chick-embryo-adapted-rabies-virus]] - 1941 Tier 1 paper recording serial propagation of rabies strains in chick embryos with CNS pathology, altered host pathogenicity, and survivor challenge-immunity endpoints; an embryo-adaptation system, not natural transmission.

### Commentary and critique
- [[sources/pasteurs-researches-on-rabies]] - Tier 2 near-contemporary 1886 BMJ report pointer; its page records that the raw note is only a stub and the report text has not been extracted, so it functions as an evidentiary placeholder.
- [[sources/louis-pasteurs-unethical-rabies-fraud]] - Tier 3 ViroLIEgy polemic arguing that Pasteur's rabies program was ethically and evidentiarily compromised; its page records that it cannot settle a misconduct conclusion on its own.
- [[sources/from-pasteur-to-panic-fear-fraud-and-the-fight-for-historical-truth]] - Tier 3 interview-style commentary presenting Mike Stone's revisionist reading of Pasteur and infectious-disease history; its page records it as agenda-setting rather than evidentiary.
- [[sources/rabies-the-virus-of-fear]] - Tier 3 essay arguing that rabies history, diagnosis, and vaccination rest on fear and weak attribution, including critique of Pasteur's rabies work; its page records that its conclusions are stronger than the raw note can justify.
- [[sources/the-germ-hypothesis-part-2]] - Tier 3 critique that uses Pasteur mainly as background contrast while arguing that later Koch-centered proof claims outran the experiments.

## Open questions
- No Pasteur primary record is in this vault: the 1886 BMJ pointer is an unextracted stub, and the Tier 1 rabies corpus mapped by the ladder begins with 1925 non-Pasteur experiments, so no claim about Pasteur's own experiments, treatment series, or outcomes is experimentally supported here yet.
- Pasteur's laboratory notebooks and original treatment case records are not in the vault; the dispute between an unextracted near-contemporary pointer and Tier 3 polemics therefore remains open.
