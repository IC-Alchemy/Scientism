# Empirical Evidence

## Definition
Empirical evidence is evidence grounded in observation, measurement, or experiment. In this vault, the term is used narrowly: an empirical observation is not the same thing as the interpretation later built on top of it.

## Key distinction
- Observation: what was measured or seen
- Experiment: what question the setup was capable of answering
- Interpretation: what broader meaning is claimed afterward

This distinction is why empirical evidence connects directly to [[concepts/inferential-leap]].

## What this wiki currently concludes
- Empirical evidence is necessary but not self-interpreting.
- The strength of an empirical claim depends on controls, endpoint clarity, repeatability, and scope.
- A source can contain real empirical observations while still drawing conclusions that go beyond them.
