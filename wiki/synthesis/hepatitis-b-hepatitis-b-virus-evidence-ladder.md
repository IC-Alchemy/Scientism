# Hepatitis B to Hepatitis B Virus Evidence Ladder

> [!warning] Warning
> This page is a research synthesis, not medical advice. It does not make exposure, testing, vaccination, or treatment recommendations.

## Current answer
The hepatitis B row can now move from candidate status to a started evidence ladder. Two full-text primary sources were obtained in this pass, but they sit at different rungs.

[[sources/krugman-giles-hammond-1967-two-types-infectious-hepatitis]] supports the pre-HAV/HBV syndrome-split rung: two hepatitis-producing inoculum patterns at Willowbrook differed by incubation, SGOT duration, thymol turbidity, contact spread, and rechallenge immunity. It does not identify HBV particles.

[[sources/acs-sells-purcell-1987-hepatitis-b-virus-transfected-hepg2-chimpanzees]] supports a high-rung experimental claim: virions produced from cloned HBV DNA in transfected Hep G2-derived cells produced HBV infection markers and hepatitis endpoints in two intravenously inoculated chimpanzees. That is stronger than mere antigen detection or association, but it is still a chimpanzee intravenous challenge model.

As of 2026-11-11 (run 19) the chain also carries an intervention rung with human disease endpoints: [[sources/coutinho-1983-heat-inactivated-hbv-vaccine-placebo-rct]] - a randomised placebo-controlled double-blind trial in which three 3-microgram doses of a heat-inactivated plasma-derived HBsAg vaccine cut life-table attack rates at 645 days for clinical hepatitis B (1.5% vs 7.7%) and for all serologically defined HBV infections (4.8% vs 23.8%, 79.7% protective efficacy) in 800 high-risk homosexual men against an aluminium-phosphate-matched albumin placebo. This is intervention evidence consistent with an infectious-agent hypothesis for hepatitis B; it does not by itself reconstruct the natural-transmission rung, and the "no serious side effects" claim is bounded by the trial's size and window.

The earlier rungs remain acquisition-sensitive. The vault should not silently fill them from abstracts, database records, or later summaries.

## Current manuscript rule for hepatitis B
Hepatitis B should be presented as an unusually high-rung but still endpoint-bound case.

- Acs et al. 1987 supports molecular-clone/cell-produced virion infectivity in chimpanzees after intravenous inoculation.
- The experiment includes infection markers, liver-enzyme response, liver histology, and recovered viral DNA matching the input pattern.
- Coutinho et al. 1983 supplies the intervention rung: a placebo-controlled human trial with disease (ALT-confirmed hepatitis B) and infection endpoints, consistent with an infectious-agent hypothesis for hepatitis B while leaving the ordinary-route transmission rung unreconstructed.
- Krugman, Giles, and Hammond 1967 supports a pre-HAV/HBV clinical, biochemical, epidemiologic, and immunologic split between MS-1/IH-like and MS-2/SH-like hepatitis patterns.
- The Australia-antigen particle detection and early human-plasma/chimpanzee transmission rungs remain acquisition gaps; their papers now have pointer-level pages ([[sources/dane-1970-australia-antigen-virus-like-particles]], [[sources/maynard-1972-experimental-chimpanzee-hepatitis-b]], [[sources/barker-1973-transmission-type-b-hepatitis-chimpanzees]]) so the queue is findable, but no rung content was filled from abstracts or summaries.
- The ordinary human route and transmission ecology should not be inferred solely from a two-chimpanzee intravenous challenge.

The manuscript may therefore upgrade HBV from a candidate row into a started syndrome-split plus molecular-clone challenge example. It should not claim that the full HBV attribution ladder has been reconstructed here.
