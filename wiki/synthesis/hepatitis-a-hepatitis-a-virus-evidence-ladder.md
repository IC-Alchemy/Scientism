# Hepatitis A to Hepatitis A Virus Evidence Ladder

> Warning: This page is a research synthesis, not medical advice. It does not make diagnosis, vaccination, exposure-management, or treatment recommendations.

## Current answer
The hepatitis A -> hepatitis A virus row can now move from candidate status to a started evidence ladder. The full-text set in this pass supports a partial but useful chain: CR326 marmoset propagation linked to human hepatitis A by neutralization and human sera, particle and tissue characterization of CR326, and later in vitro propagation in marmoset liver and FRhK6 cells.

- [[sources/provost-ittensohn-villarejos-1973-cr326-hepatitis-a-neutralization]] supports a marmoset-infectivity and human-serology neutralization bridge.
- [[sources/provost-wolanski-miller-1975-cr326-hepatitis-a-dimensions]] supports particle morphology, density, physical-chemical properties, infected-liver localization, and serologic identity.
- [[sources/provost-hilleman-1979-human-hepatitis-a-cell-culture]] supports noncytopathic cell-culture propagation with serologic identity and marmoset infectivity confirmation.

This ladder is not complete. The canonical 1973 Science immune-electron-microscopy detection paper, the 1975 chimpanzee infection paper, and several Oxford/JID papers could not be used for manuscript-strength claims in this pass because full text was not obtained or was blocked. They remain acquisition gaps.

## Evidence ladder
| Rung | Current evidence in hand | What it can answer | What it cannot answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marmoset propagation and human serology | [[sources/provost-ittensohn-villarejos-1973-cr326-hepatitis-a-neutralization]] | CR326 infectivity in marmosets was neutralized by convalescent hepatitis A sera and immune globulin, but not by a hepatitis-B-specific response. | Ordinary human transmission or direct human challenge with marmoset-propagated virus. |
| Particle characterization and tissue localization | [[sources/provost-wolanski-miller-1975-cr326-hepatitis-a-dimensions]] | CR326 appeared as 27 nm particles, had density about 1.34, was seen in marmoset liver cytoplasm, reacted with hepatitis A antisera, and showed an enterovirus-like stability profile. | Human stool detection, human clinical causation, or cell-culture propagation. |
| Cell-culture propagation | [[sources/provost-hilleman-1979-human-hepatitis-a-cell-culture]] | CR326 could be serially propagated in marmoset liver explant cultures and FRhK6 cells, with serologic identity and marmoset infectivity confirmation. | Human natural-route causation, clinical severity, or population transmission. |
| Human acute-case detection | Acquisition gap | The 1973 Science IEM paper is the canonical lead for virus-like particles in acute hepatitis A stools. | No full-text claim is strengthened here until the paper is obtained. |
| Chimpanzee challenge and tissue shedding | Acquisition gap | 1975-1977 chimpanzee papers likely address infection, liver/bile/stool antigen, and pathology. | No supported chimpanzee rung is created in this pass. |

## Current manuscript rule for hepatitis A and hepatitis A virus
Hepatitis A should be presented as a partial evidence ladder:

- Provost et al. 1973 answers a CR326 marmoset neutralization and human-serology identity question.
- Provost et al. 1975 answers a particle morphology, density, stability, and infected-liver localization question.
- Provost and Hilleman 1979 answers a cell-culture propagation and marmoset infectivity confirmation question.
- Feinstone/Kapikian/Purcell 1973 and the chimpanzee papers remain full-text acquisition gaps in this pass.

The manuscript may upgrade hepatitis A from candidate status to a started ladder, but only for the CR326 marmoset/characterization/cell-culture chain. It should say that human acute-stool IEM detection and chimpanzee challenge/tissue shedding are not reconstructed here from full text.
