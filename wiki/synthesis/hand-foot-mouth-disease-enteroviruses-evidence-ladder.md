# Hand, Foot, and Mouth Disease to Enteroviruses Evidence Ladder

> [!warning] Warning
> This page is a research synthesis, not medical advice. It does not make diagnosis, exposure-management, infection-control, or treatment recommendations.

## Current answer
The HFMD -> Coxsackievirus A16 / enterovirus 71 row can move from open status to a started evidence ladder. The full-text set in this pass supports four distinct rungs:

- [[sources/alsop-flewett-foster-1960-hand-foot-mouth-disease-birmingham]] supports an early clinical outbreak and Coxsackie A16 lesion-isolation rung.
- [[sources/brown-oleary-1974-fluorescent-antibody-hfmd]] supports a Coxsackie A16 serology and contact-response rung.
- [[sources/ishimaru-1980-hand-foot-mouth-enterovirus-71-cns-complications]] supports an EV71 outbreak, virus-typing, and CNS-complication association rung.
- [[sources/li-2014-tree-shrew-coxsackievirus-a16-experimental-infection]] supports a Coxsackie A16 tree-shrew animal-model rung with mock controls and tissue-distribution endpoints.
- [[sources/zhu-2014-sinovac-ev71-hfmd-rct]] supports an intervention row with a virologically confirmed clinical disease endpoint: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled phase 3, 10,077 children 6-35 months (Jiangsu), Vero-cell inactivated EV71 (C4) vs aluminum-hydroxide placebo, EV71-associated HFMD/herpangina efficacy 94.8% (hospitalization 0 vs 24, severe 0 vs 8), no efficacy against coxsackievirus A16 or all-cause HFMD.
- [[sources/li-2014-imb-ev71-hfmd-rct]] replicates that intervention rung on a second platform: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled phase 3, 12,000 children 6-71 months (Guangxi), human-diploid-cell KMB17 inactivated EV71 (C4) vs aluminum-hydroxide placebo, EV71-associated HFMD efficacy 97.4% (CI 92.9-99.0), both severe cases and the one EV71 death in the placebo arm, again no CA16 effect.

The current full-text set is enough to show that HFMD is not a single endpoint. Coxsackie A16 lesion isolation, Coxsackie A16 serology, EV71 outbreak association, CNS complications, animal-model systemic infection, and vaccine-intervention disease reduction answer different questions.

|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical syndrome and lesion isolation | [[sources/alsop-flewett-foster-1960-hand-foot-mouth-disease-birmingham]] | Coxsackie A16 was isolated from hand-vesicle fluid in one clinically typical HFMD case during the Birmingham outbreak. | That every outbreak case or every HFMD-like syndrome was Coxsackie A16 disease. |
| Serology and contacts | [[sources/brown-oleary-1974-fluorescent-antibody-hfmd]] | Coxsackie A16 fluorescent-antibody responses tracked clinical cases and some family or school contacts, including subclinical infection markers. | Acute causation in every individual, transmission direction, or EV71 attribution. |
| EV71 outbreak and CNS complications | [[sources/ishimaru-1980-hand-foot-mouth-enterovirus-71-cns-complications]] | EV71 was associated with HFMD outbreaks that had frequent CNS complications in the reported Japanese outbreaks. | Mechanism of neuroinvasion, causal attribution for every neurologic event, or universal EV71 severity. |
| Animal model | [[sources/li-2014-tree-shrew-coxsackievirus-a16-experimental-infection]] | Coxsackie A16 GX18 could establish systemic infection markers in tree shrews after nasal-spray inoculation with mock controls. | Human lesion tropism, ordinary pediatric transmission, or natural HFMD disease reproduction. |
| Intervention (disease endpoint) | [[sources/zhu-2014-sinovac-ev71-hfmd-rct]]; replicated 2026-11-11 (run 23) by [[sources/li-2014-imb-ev71-hfmd-rct]] | Blocking EV71 with an inactivated C4 vaccine reduced virologically confirmed EV71-associated HFMD: 94.8% (87.2-97.9) in 10,077 Jiangsu children 6-35 months (Vero-cell platform, 400 U antigen vs aluminum-hydroxide placebo quoted) with EV71-hospitalization 0 vs 24 and neurologic-severe HFMD 0 vs 8; and 97.4% (92.9-99.0) in 12,000 Guangxi children 6-71 months (diploid-cell KMB17 platform, 100 U antigen vs aluminum-hydroxide placebo quoted) with both severe cases and the one EV71 death in the placebo arm. Both trials simultaneously carry a pathogen-specificity negative: no efficacy against coxsackievirus A16 (6.6% / 11.1%) and only ~20%/~49% against all-cause HFMD, because EV71 caused only a fraction of cases. | All-cause HFMD burden, transmission or carriage, other EV71 genogroups (both vaccines are C4; B3/B4 cross-neutralization untested clinically), protection duration beyond the 11-14-month windows, and systemic-safety inference against a true inert placebo (both comparators contained aluminum hydroxide). |

## Current manuscript rule for HFMD and enteroviruses
HFMD should be presented as a syndrome with multiple enterovirus attribution rungs:

- Coxsackie A16 lesion isolation is a lesion-material recovery endpoint.
- Coxsackie A16 serology is an immune-response and contact endpoint.
- EV71 outbreak evidence is a virus-typing and complication-association endpoint.
- Tree-shrew infection is an animal-model endpoint.
- As of 2026-11-11 (run 23, queue item 44), EV71 has a placebo-controlled disease-endpoint intervention rung replicated across two platforms: [[sources/zhu-2014-sinovac-ev71-hfmd-rct]] and [[sources/li-2014-imb-ev71-hfmd-rct]] - intervention evidence consistent with the infectious-agent hypothesis for the EV71 share of HFMD, with both trials' no-effect-on-CA16 rows showing the effect is EV71-specific rather than nonspecific. The manuscript should also say that neither trial reduced all-cause HFMD proportionally (EV71 was only ~5% of observed HFMD/herpangina cases in the Sinovac area-season), that both placebos contained aluminum hydroxide (adjuvant-matched, not inert), that transmission, carriage, and duration claims are not answered by these experiments, and that the third (Beijing Vigoo, Lancet 2013) trial remains abstract-only in this vault.

The manuscript may say that the current full-text set supports Coxsackie A16 and EV71 as experimentally documented HFMD-associated enteroviruses. It should also say that sparse early isolation, serology, outbreak association, and animal-model systemic spread do not collapse into proof that every HFMD presentation, every neurologic complication, or every transmission route has been settled.
