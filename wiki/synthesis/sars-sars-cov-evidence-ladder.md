# SARS to SARS-CoV Evidence Ladder

> [!warning] Warning
> This page is a research synthesis, not medical advice. It does not make diagnosis, infection-control, exposure-management, or treatment recommendations.

## Current answer
The SARS -> SARS-CoV row can move from open status to a started evidence ladder. The full-text set in this pass supports a chain from patient association and virus isolation to experimental macaque pathogenicity:

- [[sources/peiris-2003-coronavirus-possible-cause-sars]] supports a Hong Kong SARS patient-series association, virus isolation, coronavirus morphology, RT-PCR, and paired-serology rung.
- [[sources/drosten-2003-novel-coronavirus-sars-patients]] supports independent virus identification, sequence characterization, SARS-CoV-specific PCR assays, specimen viral-load detection, and early serology.
- [[sources/kuiken-2003-newly-discovered-coronavirus-primary-cause-sars]] supports multicentre patient association plus cynomolgus macaque challenge, lung pathology, viral localization, RT-PCR, and partial re-isolation.

The strongest causal rung in hand is the Kuiken macaque experiment. It remains model-bound: four macaques, high-dose combined intratracheal/intranasal/conjunctival inoculation, day-6 necropsy, and no sham-inoculated macaque comparator.

## Evidence ladder
| Rung | Current evidence in hand | What it can answer | What it cannot answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical syndrome and patient association | [[sources/peiris-2003-coronavirus-possible-cause-sars]]; [[sources/drosten-2003-novel-coronavirus-sars-patients]]; [[sources/kuiken-2003-newly-discovered-coronavirus-primary-cause-sars]] | SARS patients in the tested series often had SARS-CoV RNA, serologic response, or both, while specified controls were negative. | Causal sufficiency, ordinary route, or all SARS-like illness mechanisms. |
| Culture and isolation | [[sources/peiris-2003-coronavirus-possible-cause-sars]]; [[sources/drosten-2003-novel-coronavirus-sars-patients]] | Coronavirus-compatible agents could be isolated in cell culture from patient material and genetically characterized. | Disease reproduction or natural transmission. |
| Serology | [[sources/peiris-2003-coronavirus-possible-cause-sars]]; [[sources/drosten-2003-novel-coronavirus-sars-patients]] | Paired sera in the Peiris series and selected Drosten cases showed immune response to the isolate or infected culture. | Necessity, sufficiency, route, or proof that every SARS case was SARS-CoV disease. |
| Multicentre exclusion and comparison | [[sources/kuiken-2003-newly-discovered-coronavirus-primary-cause-sars]] | SARS-CoV was diagnosed in 329 of 436 SARS patients, while human metapneumovirus and other pathogens were less frequent or sporadic in that multicentre set. | A perfectly uniform case definition or proof that cofactors never mattered. |
| Animal challenge and pathology | [[sources/kuiken-2003-newly-discovered-coronavirus-primary-cause-sars]] | Four cynomolgus macaques exposed to high-dose SARS-CoV by combined routes developed respiratory viral shedding; three of four developed diffuse alveolar damage with viral localization in pneumonic lung. | Ordinary human natural-route causation, route-specific dose, or sham-controlled symptom attribution. |
| Re-isolation and tissue recovery | [[sources/kuiken-2003-newly-discovered-coronavirus-primary-cause-sars]] | SARS-CoV was isolated from selected respiratory samples and from lung/kidney or lung tissue in some infected macaques; RT-PCR detected viral material in lung and other tissues. | A complete time-course, population transmission, or full separation of infectious virus from RNA-only findings in every positive tissue. |

## Current manuscript rule for SARS and SARS-CoV
SARS should be presented as a multi-rung evidence ladder:

- Peiris et al. 2003 answers patient association, virus isolation, EM morphology, RT-PCR, serology, and control-negativity questions in a Hong Kong case series.
- Drosten et al. 2003 answers independent virus identification, sequence, PCR assay, viral-load, and selected serology questions.
- Kuiken et al. 2003 answers multicentre association and model-specific macaque challenge/pathology/re-isolation questions.
- Ksiazek et al. 2003, Rota et al. 2003, and Fouchier et al. 2003 now have abstract-level source pages ([[sources/ksiazek-2003-novel-coronavirus-urbani-strain]], [[sources/rota-2003-sars-cov-genome-characterization]], [[sources/fouchier-2003-kochs-postulates-sars-macaques]]) but remain full-text acquisition leads at manuscript strength.

The manuscript may say that the current full-text set supports a strong SARS-CoV attribution chain, including an animal challenge rung. It should also say that the animal challenge used artificial combined-route exposure in four macaques and lacked a sham-inoculated macaque comparator, so ordinary human natural-route causation and route-specific transmission should not be silently imported from that experiment.
