# Viral Disease Causation Evidence Ladder

> [!warning] Warning
> This page is a methodological synthesis, not medical advice and not a proof page for any single virus-disease pair.

## Current answer
The current vault evidence does not support a blanket statement that viral disease causation has either been globally proven or globally refuted. The better answer is disease-specific and endpoint-specific.

For each claimed viral disease, the vault should ask which rung of evidence is actually in hand and what question that rung can answer.

## Why a ladder is needed
The existing [[synthesis/virus-disease-attribution-audit-worklist]] shows that many diseases have candidate foundational papers, but candidate status is not the same as completed evidence review. The central risk is endpoint compression: a source may show detection, culture, serology, animal disease, or intervention effect, while later summaries retell the result as simple causal closure.

[[sources/rivers-1937-viruses-and-kochs-postulates]] is the key historical bridge. Rivers argued that viruses do not have to satisfy Koch's bacteriology rules literally, but he still required more than mere association: the virus must be shown to be causally involved rather than incidental or coexisting.

[[sources/fredericks-relman-1996-sequence-based-identification-microbial-pathogens]] extends the same caution into molecular evidence. Sequence evidence can be useful, but a sequence hit is not the same as disease causation unless it is tied to controls, localization, timing, biological plausibility, and the disease endpoint.

[[sources/jefferson-2022-hierarchical-framework-transmission-causality-respiratory-viruses]] applies the same problem to respiratory-virus transmission: weak detection evidence and strong transmission evidence should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Evidence ladder
| Rung | Evidence type | What it can answer | What it cannot answer by itself |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Clinical syndrome description | Whether a named disease pattern was observed. | Whether a virus caused it. |
| 2 | Microscopy, antigen, PCR, RT-qPCR, or sequencing detection | Whether viral material or a viral signal was detected in sampled material. | Whether the detected material was infectious, sufficient, or causal. |
| 3 | Culture, propagation, or isolation workflow | Whether an agent can be propagated or characterized under laboratory conditions. | Whether it caused the original disease in ordinary conditions. |
| 4 | Serology or immune response | Whether the host mounted an immune response associated with the agent. | Whether the agent was necessary, sufficient, or non-incidental. |
| 5 | Animal challenge or experimental exposure | Whether the agent or material can produce disease-like endpoints in a specified host by a specified route. | Whether ordinary human natural-route causation is settled. |
| 6 | Re-isolation or recovery from the experimental host | Whether the agent can be recovered after experimental infection. | Whether this recapitulates natural disease ecology. |
| 7 | Controlled human challenge | Whether exposure under a specified protocol can produce defined clinical or laboratory endpoints. | Whether population-level disease patterns, cofactors, and all real-world transmission are explained. |
| 8 | Prospective cohort, outbreak, or transmission-chain evidence | Whether exposure, infection markers, and disease endpoints align over time in humans. | Whether all alternative causes, cofactors, or measurement artifacts are excluded. |
| 9 | Intervention evidence | Whether blocking, removing, or modifying the agent changes a disease endpoint. | Whether the whole causal story is one-factor, sufficient, or universal. |
| 10 | Replicated cross-method convergence | Whether multiple independent methods point to the same causal model. | It still does not justify ignoring endpoint limits or unfavorable findings. |

## How this applies to current vault rows
### Stronger experimental ladders
- Hepatitis A now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/hepatitis-a-hepatitis-a-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports a CR326 marmoset-infectivity and human-serology neutralization bridge through [[sources/provost-ittensohn-villarejos-1973-cr326-hepatitis-a-neutralization]], particle morphology/density/tissue-localization through [[sources/provost-wolanski-miller-1975-cr326-hepatitis-a-dimensions]], and noncytopathic cell-culture propagation plus marmoset infectivity confirmation through [[sources/provost-hilleman-1979-human-hepatitis-a-cell-culture]]. Human acute-stool IEM detection and chimpanzee challenge/tissue-shedding papers remain full-text acquisition gaps in this pass.
- Hepatitis B now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/hepatitis-b-hepatitis-b-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports a pre-HAV/HBV syndrome-split rung through [[sources/krugman-giles-hammond-1967-two-types-infectious-hepatitis]] and molecular-clone/cell-produced HBV virion infectivity in two intravenously inoculated chimpanzees through [[sources/acs-sells-purcell-1987-hepatitis-b-virus-transfected-hepg2-chimpanzees]], while the 1970 Australia-antigen particle, 1972 human-plasma chimpanzee challenge, and 1973 chimpanzee transmission/passaging papers remain full-text acquisition gaps in this pass.
- Hepatitis C now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/hepatitis-c-hepatitis-c-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports molecular identification through [[sources/choo-kuo-weiner-1989-cdna-clone-non-a-non-b-hcv-genome]], recombinant-antigen antibody-assay validation through [[sources/kuo-choo-alter-1989-assay-circulating-antibodies-hcv]], prospective transfusion-recipient serology and donor linkage through [[sources/alter-purcell-shih-1989-hcv-antibody-transfusion-recipients]], and direct intrahepatic chimpanzee molecular-clone infectivity through [[sources/yanagi-purcell-emerson-bukh-1997-hcv-infectious-cdna-clone-chimpanzee]]. The 1984 non-A/non-B chimpanzee passage paper and 1999 transmissible-virion clone paper remain full-text acquisition gaps.
- Shingles/VZV latency now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/shingles-vzv-reactivation-evidence-ladder]], with source-page extraction for ganglionic latency, neuronal localization, latent transcription, and active ganglionitis endpoints.

### Transmission-boundary ladders
- Cold sores, genital herpes, and shingles now have dedicated ladder pages: [[synthesis/cold-sores-hsv1-evidence-ladder]], [[synthesis/genital-herpes-hsv-evidence-ladder]], and [[synthesis/shingles-vzv-reactivation-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports HSV-1 oral recovery/shedding, genital HSV lesion culture and asymptomatic shedding/transmission cases, and VZV ganglionic latency/reactivation rungs. The key boundary is that PCR shedding, culture recovery, case-level transmission, and ganglionic detection do not answer the same causal question.
- Bronchiolitis now has a dedicated RSV ladder page, [[synthesis/bronchiolitis-rsv-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports early CCA-related virus isolation through [[sources/chanock-roizman-myers-1957-cca-rsv-isolation-characterization]], early serology/epidemiology with control limits through [[sources/chanock-finberg-1957-cca-rsv-epidemiology-infants]], diagnostic bronchiolitis association through [[sources/cradock-watson-1971-rapid-diagnosis-rsv-immunofluorescence]], cotton-rat respiratory pathology through [[sources/prince-1978-pathogenesis-rsv-infection-cotton-rats]], and adult route-specific infection through [[sources/hall-1981-infectivity-rsv-various-routes-inoculation]]. Nasopharyngeal detection, adult inoculation, and cotton-rat pathology should not be collapsed into direct infant bronchiolitis causation.
- Rotavirus now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/rotavirus-gastroenteritis-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports pediatric mucosal antigen localization through [[sources/davidson-1975-rotavirus-immunofluorescence-duodenal-mucosa]], hospitalized-child clinical association through [[sources/mccormack-1982-clinical-features-rotavirus-gastroenteritis]], adult oral challenge through [[sources/kapikian-1983-oral-human-rotavirus-volunteers]], and culture/serotyping through [[sources/wyatt-1983-direct-isolation-human-rotaviruses-cell-culture-serotypes]]. Pediatric tissue association, adult challenge, and culture are not interchangeable endpoints. The chain now also carries an intervention rung: [[sources/vesikari-2006-pentavalent-rotavirus-rest-rct]] (REST trial, ~68,000 infants, placebo-controlled, lab-confirmed G1-G4 gastroenteritis and severity-scored severe disease endpoints) supplies rung-9 evidence for rotavirus, without answering natural transmission or all-cause health outcomes.
- Norovirus now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/norovirus-gastroenteritis-outbreaks-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports Norwalk-agent particle/serology through [[sources/kapikian-1972-immune-electron-microscopy-norwalk-agent]], adult human challenge and shedding through [[sources/atmar-2008-norwalk-virus-shedding-experimental-human-infection]], outbreak attribution through [[sources/arness-2000-norwalk-like-gastroenteritis-army-trainees]], and human enteroid replication through [[sources/ettayebi-2016-human-norovirus-replication-enteroids]]. PCR shedding, challenge illness, outbreak epidemiology, and culture replication should remain separate rungs.
- Hand, foot, and mouth disease now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/hand-foot-mouth-disease-enteroviruses-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports Coxsackie A16 lesion isolation through [[sources/alsop-flewett-foster-1960-hand-foot-mouth-disease-birmingham]], Coxsackie A16 serology and contact responses through [[sources/brown-oleary-1974-fluorescent-antibody-hfmd]], EV71 outbreak/CNS association through [[sources/ishimaru-1980-hand-foot-mouth-enterovirus-71-cns-complications]], and Coxsackie A16 tree-shrew model infection through [[sources/li-2014-tree-shrew-coxsackievirus-a16-experimental-infection]]. The main boundary is that lesion isolation, serology, outbreak association, and animal-model systemic infection are separate endpoints.
- MERS now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/mers-mers-cov-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports index-case isolation, culture, sequencing, and limited serology through [[sources/zaki-2012-novel-coronavirus-pneumonia-saudi-arabia]], model-specific rhesus-macaque lower-respiratory challenge and pathology through [[sources/dewit-2013-mers-cov-rhesus-macaque-lower-respiratory-infection]], and dromedary-camel upper-respiratory replication/shedding ecology through [[sources/adney-2014-mers-cov-dromedary-camel-shedding]]. The main boundary is that a single fatal human case with bacterial coinfections, a high-dose combined-route macaque model, and a high-dose combined-route camel model should not be compressed into ordinary human natural-route causation.
- SARS now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/sars-sars-cov-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports patient-series association, culture/isolation, EM, RT-PCR, serology, and control-negativity through [[sources/peiris-2003-coronavirus-possible-cause-sars]] and [[sources/drosten-2003-novel-coronavirus-sars-patients]], plus multicentre association and cynomolgus macaque challenge/pathology/re-isolation through [[sources/kuiken-2003-newly-discovered-coronavirus-primary-cause-sars]]. The strongest experimental rung is still model-bound: four macaques, high-dose combined intratracheal/intranasal/conjunctival exposure, day-6 necropsy, and no sham-inoculated macaque comparator.
- Smallpox now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/smallpox-variola-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports artificial variola inoculation and route comparison in nonhuman primates through [[sources/brinckerhoff-tyzzer-1906-experimental-variola-monkeys]], mouse nasal-route and lung-recovery boundaries through [[sources/nelson-1939-variola-virus-nasal-instillation-mice]] and [[sources/nelson-1940-variola-vaccinia-survival-mouse-lungs]], egg propagation and stability through [[sources/nelson-1943-stability-variola-virus-embryonated-eggs]] and [[sources/oya-1956-vaccinia-variola-one-day-hen-egg-growth]], monkey contact/aerosol transmission through [[sources/noble-rich-1969-smallpox-contact-aerosol-macaca-irus]], and simian-reservoir limits through [[sources/noble-1970-simian-reservoir-smallpox-monkeys]]. Ordinary human smallpox transmission and purified-virus human challenge remain separate rungs not reconstructed in this pass.
- Measles now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/measles-measles-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set preserves failed blood-inoculation boundaries in monkeys and human volunteers through [[sources/sellards-wentworth-1919-monkeys-blood-measles-patients]] and [[sources/sellards-1919-man-blood-measles-patients]], respiratory-material monkey challenge and passage through [[sources/blake-trask-1921-measles-monkey-susceptibility]], experimental syndrome/pathology definitions through [[sources/blake-trask-1921-measles-symptomatology-pathology-monkeys]], tissue-culture isolation and serology through [[sources/enders-peebles-1954-cytopathogenic-agents-measles-tissue-culture]], and plaque/replication behavior through [[sources/rapp-1964-plaque-differentiation-measles-virus]]. Ordinary human respiratory transmission and culture-passaged human challenge remain separate rungs not reconstructed in this pass.
- Rabies now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/rabies-rabies-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports artificial rabbit masseter inoculation and neural-spread pathology through [[sources/goodpasture-1925-rabies-neural-transmission-rabbits-negri-bodies]], chick-embryo propagation and altered host pathogenicity through [[sources/dawson-1941-chick-embryo-adapted-rabies-virus]], infected-brain particle morphology through [[sources/matsumoto-1963-electron-microscope-rabies-virus-mouse-brain]], BHK/21 tissue-culture replication through [[sources/hummeler-koprowski-wiktor-1967-rabies-virus-tissue-culture]], street/fixed strain pathology differences through [[sources/miyamoto-matsumoto-1967-pathogenesis-street-fixed-rabies]], and oral/nasal route boundaries through [[sources/charlton-casey-1979-experimental-rabies-skunks-route-exposure]] and [[sources/shirakawa-2007-cats-fed-rabid-mouse-brains]]. Ordinary bite transmission, human clinical causation, and post-exposure prophylaxis evidence remain separate rungs not reconstructed in this pass.
- Influenza now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/influenza-influenza-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set preserves Rosenau's pre-virus human transmission-failure boundary alongside [[sources/smith-andrewes-laidlaw-1933-virus-obtained-from-influenza-patients]] for human-case filtrate and ferret passage and [[sources/shope-francis-1936-susceptibility-of-swine-to-human-influenza-virus]] for swine susceptibility and contact-transfer limits.
- COVID-19 currently has an endpoint-specific audit in [[synthesis/sars-cov-2-covid-causation-audit]]. The key boundary is that PCR and RT-qPCR are detection endpoints, animal inoculation is model-specific pathogenicity, and the audited human challenge evidence is uncontrolled challenge-model evidence rather than sham-controlled clinical causation proof.
- Influenza now has a full-text modern controlled-human-challenge rung (rung 7) through [[sources/memoli-2015-h1n1pdm09-human-challenge-model-validation]] (dose-finding, no sham arm, 69% MMID at 10^7 TCID50) and [[sources/han-2021-cr6261-placebo-controlled-h1n1pdm09-challenge]] (randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial; virus-only arm 69% MMID, 88% confirmed infection). The boundary stays sharp: inoculation-route disease reproduction is not natural-route transmission, which for influenza remains unreconstructed, and PCR-detected shedding is not demonstrated infectiousness.
- Poliomyelitis now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/poliomyelitis-poliovirus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports route/tissue-recovery and tissue-culture cultivation rungs through [[sources/sabin-olitsky-1938-fate-of-nasally-instilled-poliomyelitis-virus]] and [[sources/enders-weller-robbins-1949-cultivation-lansing-strain-human-embryonic-tissues]], but the vault still lacks a complete natural-route and direct patient-isolation reconstruction.
- Mumps now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/mumps-mumps-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports rhesus-monkey parotid-duct inoculation, chick-embryo isolation, complement-fixation identification, induced human exposure, serologic response, and saliva virus-isolation rungs through [[sources/johnson-goodpasture-1934-investigation-etiology-mumps]], [[sources/leymaster-ward-1947-direct-isolation-mumps-virus-chick-embryos]], and [[sources/henle-henle-wendell-rosenberg-1948-induced-mumps-infections-virus-isolation]]. It still separates those endpoints from ordinary community transmission and sham-controlled human clinical causation.
- Rubella now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/rubella-rubella-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports acute throat-washing recovery, tissue-culture propagation, paired-serum neutralization, and grivet-monkey inoculation rungs through [[sources/parkman-buescher-artenstein-1962-recovery-rubella-virus-army-recruits]], [[sources/weller-neva-1962-propagation-cytopathic-agents-rubella-like-illness]], and [[sources/sigurdardottir-givan-rozee-rhodes-1963-rubella-toronto-monkey-transmission]]. It still separates acute rubella from congenital-rubella and ordinary-transmission claims.
- Chickenpox now has a dedicated ladder page, [[synthesis/chickenpox-varicella-zoster-virus-evidence-ladder]]. The current full-text set supports varicella lesion-fluid culture, fatal-case tissue recovery, and varicella/zoster cultural-similarity rungs through [[sources/weller-1953-serial-propagation-varicella-herpes-zoster-agents]], [[sources/cheatham-dolan-dower-weller-1956-fatal-varicella-necropsy-virus-isolation]], and [[sources/weller-witton-bell-1958-varicella-zoster-isolation-propagation-culture]]. It still separates chickenpox attribution from zoster latency/reactivation and ordinary transmission.

## Current unresolved assessment
Under this vault's evidence standard, "virus causes disease" should not be accepted or rejected as one global proposition. The phrase has to be expanded into a named disease, named virus, named study design, exposure route, comparator or control, endpoint, result, and limitation.

The current evidence in hand supports a methodological conclusion: viral causation claims often rest on multiple rungs that are rhetorically compressed after the fact. That does not prove the claims false. It means each disease row needs a paper-by-paper ladder reconstruction before it can be marked supported, contradicted, or unresolved.
