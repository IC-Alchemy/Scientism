# Kaposi Sarcoma to Human Herpesvirus 8 Evidence Ladder

> [!warning] Warning
> This page is a research synthesis, not medical advice. It does not make treatment, prevention, screening, or antiviral-use recommendations.

## Current answer
The Kaposi sarcoma -> HHV-8/KSHV row can now move from candidate status to a started evidence ladder. The current full-text set supports molecular discovery, lesion association, cross-status detection, lesion-versus-normal tissue contrast, viral-genome characterization, infected-cell-line evidence, and serology:

- [[sources/chang-cesarman-pessin-1994-herpesvirus-like-dna-aids-kaposi]] supports discovery of novel herpesvirus-like DNA sequences strongly associated with AIDS-associated KS tissue.
- [[sources/moore-chang-1995-herpesvirus-like-dna-kaposi-hiv-nonhiv]] supports detection of the same KSHV-like marker in AIDS-associated KS, classic KS, and KS in HIV-seronegative homosexual men.
- [[sources/oneill-henson-ghorbani-1996-herpesvirus-like-sequences-kaposi-lesions]] supports lesion-concentrated PCR detection in a small AIDS-KS tissue set with matched normal tissue negative under the reported conditions.
- [[sources/moore-gao-dominguez-1996-primary-characterization-kshv]] supports characterization of the agent as a new human gamma-2 herpesvirus and adds infected-cell-line and serology endpoints.

This is a strong molecular-association and characterization ladder. It is not a complete tumor-causation ladder. The current full-text set does not prove infectious-virus isolation from ordinary KS lesions, ordinary transmission route, necessary cause, sufficient cause, or tumor initiation mechanism.

## Evidence ladder
| Rung | Current evidence in hand | What it can answer | What it cannot answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molecular discovery in KS tissue | [[sources/chang-cesarman-pessin-1994-herpesvirus-like-dna-aids-kaposi]] | Novel herpesvirus-like sequences were enriched in AIDS-KS tissue and homologous to gammaherpesvirus genes. | Discovery of a sequence marker is not causal proof or infectious-virus isolation. |
| Lesion association and controls | [[sources/chang-cesarman-pessin-1994-herpesvirus-like-dna-aids-kaposi]]; [[sources/oneill-henson-ghorbani-1996-herpesvirus-like-sequences-kaposi-lesions]] | KSHV-like DNA was common in tested KS lesions and rare or absent in many non-KS comparison tissues; matched normal tissue was negative in O'Neill et al.'s small set. | Lesion association does not prove temporal precedence, necessity, sufficiency, or tumor initiation. |
| Cross-status KS detection | [[sources/moore-chang-1995-herpesvirus-like-dna-kaposi-hiv-nonhiv]] | The KSHV-like marker appeared in AIDS-associated KS, classic KS, and KS in HIV-seronegative homosexual men. | It does not settle whether HIV status, immune suppression, geography, cofactors, or host susceptibility are required. |
| Viral characterization | [[sources/moore-gao-dominguez-1996-primary-characterization-kshv]] | KSHV was characterized as a new human gamma-2 herpesvirus with a larger genome and herpesvirus-like ORF organization. | Characterization does not equal purified virion recovery from KS lesions or experimental tumor causation. |
| Cell-line and transfer evidence | [[sources/moore-gao-dominguez-1996-primary-characterization-kshv]] | KSHV genome was maintained in naturally transformed lymphoma cell lines and could be detected transiently in some recipient cultures after exposure to infected-cell material. | The paper did not identify replication-competent virions, and transient PCR-positive recipient cultures are not equivalent to confirmed infectious transmission. |
| Serology | [[sources/moore-gao-dominguez-1996-primary-characterization-kshv]] | After EBV-reactive antibody adsorption, AIDS-KS sera had higher infected-cell-line IFA titers than HIV/AIDS controls without KS. | This limited serology does not replace full population seroepidemiology, prospective timing, or necessity testing. Gao et al. 1996 remains an acquisition gap. |
| Prospective timing and transmission | Whitby et al. 1995 and related blood/progression papers remain acquisition leads. | We do not know at manuscript strength from this pass. | Cross-sectional lesion PCR cannot substitute for prospective pre-lesion infection timing or route-specific transmission evidence. |

## Current manuscript rule for Kaposi sarcoma and HHV-8
Kaposi sarcoma should be presented as an association-heavy molecular and serologic ladder, not as a simple challenge model:

- Chang et al. 1994 answers a molecular-discovery and AIDS-KS tissue-association question.
- Moore and Chang 1995 answers a cross-status lesion-detection question.
- O'Neill et al. 1996 answers a small lesion-versus-normal tissue PCR question.
- Moore et al. 1996 answers a viral-characterization, infected-cell-line, transfer-attempt, and limited-serology question.

The manuscript may therefore upgrade HHV-8/Kaposi sarcoma from a candidate row into a started molecular-characterization evidence ladder. It should not state that necessity, sufficiency, ordinary transmission, immune-cofactor structure, or tumor initiation has been reconstructed here.
