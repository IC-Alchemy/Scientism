# Infectious Mononucleosis to Epstein-Barr Virus Evidence Ladder

> Warning: This page is a research synthesis, not medical advice. It does not make diagnostic, treatment, or prevention recommendations.

## Current answer
The infectious mononucleosis -> EBV row can now move from candidate status to a started evidence ladder. The current full-text set supports immunofluorescence/cell-culture comparison, EBV antibody association, family-contact serology, prospective seroconversion, EBV-specific IgM timing, and clinical IM endpoints:

- [[sources/klein-pearson-henle-1968-ebv-membrane-immunofluorescence-im]] supports shared EBV-linked membrane immunofluorescence behavior between IM-derived blastoid cultures and Burkitt tumor cell lines, plus post-illness MIF antibody development in IM sera.
- [[sources/nye-lambert-1973-ebv-antibody-cases-contacts-infectious-mononucleosis]] supports EBV antibody presence or development in Paul-Bunnell-positive IM cases and EBV infection markers in a subset of contacts, with clinical IM as a minority contact outcome.
- [[sources/hallee-evans-niederman-1974-west-point-infectious-mononucleosis-ebv]] supports prospective EBV seroconversion and EBV-specific IgM timing in a young-adult cohort, while showing that most EBV infections were not heterophile-positive clinical IM.

This is a stronger timing and susceptibility ladder than tumor-association rows such as EBV/Burkitt lymphoma. It is still not a purified-virus challenge or route-specific transmission ladder. EBV infection, EBV antibody, EBV-specific IgM, heterophile-positive IM, and mononucleosis-like illness remain separate endpoints.

## Evidence ladder
| Rung | Current evidence in hand | What it can answer | What it cannot answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cell-culture/immunofluorescence | [[sources/klein-pearson-henle-1968-ebv-membrane-immunofluorescence-im]] | IM-derived blastoid cultures and Burkitt lines shared EBV-linked membrane immunofluorescence behavior in the tested system, and IM sera developed MIF antibodies after illness. | Immunofluorescence is not purified-virus clinical causation or transmission proof. |
| Case serology | [[sources/nye-lambert-1973-ebv-antibody-cases-contacts-infectious-mononucleosis]] | Paul-Bunnell-positive IM cases in this study all possessed or developed EBV antibody, and most had significant antibody change. | Antibody association is not sufficiency and does not by itself identify transmission route. |
| Contact serology | [[sources/nye-lambert-1973-ebv-antibody-cases-contacts-infectious-mononucleosis]] | EBV infection or reinfection markers appeared in a subset of contacts after index IM cases, but clinical IM was a minority outcome. | Contact serology does not prove direct transmission from a particular index case to a particular contact. |
| Prospective seroconversion | [[sources/hallee-evans-niederman-1974-west-point-infectious-mononucleosis-ebv]] | EBV seroconversion prospectively preceded or accompanied clinical IM in initially EBV-antibody-negative cadets, while pre-existing antibody marked low clinical susceptibility. | It does not prove purified-virus sufficiency or route-specific transmission. |
| EBV-specific IgM | [[sources/hallee-evans-niederman-1974-west-point-infectious-mononucleosis-ebv]] | EBV-specific IgM appeared during acute clinical IM and was absent before illness and one year later. | IgM timing is an infection-marker endpoint, not a full causal mechanism. |
| Clinical endpoint boundary | [[sources/nye-lambert-1973-ebv-antibody-cases-contacts-infectious-mononucleosis]]; [[sources/hallee-evans-niederman-1974-west-point-infectious-mononucleosis-ebv]] | EBV infection was often inapparent or non-definite, and some mononucleosis-like illnesses were associated with CMV, adenovirus, or unresolved causes. | EBV infection cannot be equated automatically with every mono-like syndrome or every sore-throat illness. |
| Transmission route | Full text not yet obtained here for throat-washing/oral-excretion primary papers. | We do not know at manuscript strength from this pass. | Roommate and family-contact findings cannot by themselves define route, dose, or infectious material. |

## Current manuscript rule for infectious mononucleosis and EBV
Infectious mononucleosis should be presented as a serology-and-prospective-timing ladder:

- Klein et al. 1968 answers an EBV-linked immunofluorescence and IM-serum antibody question.
- Nye and Lambert 1973 answers a case/contact EBV antibody and family-spread question.
- Hallee et al. 1974 answers a prospective young-adult EBV seroconversion, EBV-specific IgM, and clinical IM question.

The manuscript may therefore upgrade EBV/infectious mononucleosis from a candidate row into a started evidence ladder. It should not state that purified-virus challenge, route-specific transmission, sufficiency, or all mononucleosis-like illness mechanisms have been reconstructed here.
