# Gutknecht Letter Evidence Check

Scope: This page answers the 12 questions raised in the March 24, 1995 letter [[sources/congressman-questions-aids-in-letter]].

> Warning: This page is now only a research worklist. It does not answer the questions. It tells future agents what kinds of direct experiments, cohorts, or historical documents are still needed before the wiki can answer them under the vault's evidence standard.

> Note: The letter is dated March 24, 1995. Some questions are framed around 1995 statistics or premises. The answers below should now be read as a provisional indexing pass, not as settled evidentiary conclusions.

> Warning: This page is for research analysis only. It is not medical advice.

## Question status

### 1. Transmission
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct occupational-seroconversion papers
- direct transfusion-transmission cohorts
- direct perinatal-transmission trials
- explicit endpoint naming: infection, seroconversion, AIDS diagnosis, or death

### 2. Progression without the user's listed cofactors
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- seroconverter cohorts with exposure categories clearly separated
- explicit progression endpoints
- direct evidence addressing the question's exclusions rather than broad summary claims

### 3. HIV causes AIDS
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct natural-history studies
- direct intervention studies showing outcome change when viral replication is suppressed
- clear separation between causation evidence and authority summaries

### 4. HIV-negative AIDS or ICL
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct case-series papers on idiopathic CD4 lymphocytopenia
- direct comparison of endpoint and syndrome definition versus HIV stage 3 disease

### 5. Kaposi's sarcoma distribution
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct HHV-8 papers
- direct epidemiology papers on population distribution
- clear separation of cofactor, cause, and background prevalence

### 6. Long-term survivors
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct long-term-nonprogressor cohorts
- explicit progression endpoints
- direct evidence bearing on the role, if any, of recreational drugs or AZT avoidance

### 7. Hemophiliac survival
Status: partial direct-study work exists in [[synthesis/gutknecht-letter-questions-7-9]].

Needed before the question is fully closed:
- cleaner reconstruction of the exact statistic quoted in the letter
- direct comparison of pre-HIV survival gains versus HIV-era mortality

### 8. Recreational drugs or AZT as the cause of AIDS
Status: partial direct-study work exists in [[synthesis/gutknecht-letter-questions-7-9]].

Needed before the question is fully closed:
- direct-study reconstruction of non-drug exposure cohorts
- separate historical reconstruction of the AZT timeline from primary historical documents

### 9. Ethics of prescribing AZT
Status: partial direct-study work exists in [[synthesis/gutknecht-letter-questions-7-9]].

Needed before the question is fully closed:
- broader trial set beyond the initial zidovudine studies
- explicit distinction between efficacy, toxicity, and ethics

### 10. Autoimmune precedent and immune-pathogenesis mechanism
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct mechanistic HIV pathogenesis papers
- direct studies on AIDS-defining illnesses as endpoints
- separate treatment of autoimmune-trigger questions versus immunodeficiency questions

### 11. HIV detection and cross-reactivity
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct assay-performance papers
- direct papers on false positives, specificity, confirmatory algorithms, and what was actually measured
- explicit distinction between surveillance definition and laboratory detection

### 12. Alternative-hypothesis funding
Status: unresolved in this page.

Needed before answering:
- direct grant-history review
- named awards, amounts, dates, investigators, and project abstracts
- separate treatment of general HIV research from explicit non-HIV-causation work

## What this wiki currently concludes
- This page should not currently be treated as an answer page.
- It is now only a research worklist for rebuilding the topic from direct studies.
- Questions 7-9 have the best first-pass direct-study reconstruction so far, but even those remain unresolved.
