# Shingles to VZV Reactivation Evidence Ladder

> Warning: This page is a research synthesis, not medical advice. It does not make diagnosis, treatment, exposure-management, or vaccine recommendations.

## Current answer
The shingles -> VZV reactivation row can move from candidate status to a started evidence ladder:

- [[sources/weller-witton-bell-1958-varicella-zoster-isolation-propagation-culture]] supports cultural similarity of varicella- and zoster-derived agents from lesions.
- [[sources/lungu-1995-reactivated-latent-vzv-human-dorsal-root-ganglia]] supports VZV nucleic-acid/protein detection in human dorsal-root ganglia during latent/reactivated states.
- [[sources/kennedy-1998-latent-vzv-neurons-human-trigeminal-ganglia]] supports predominant neuronal localization of latent VZV DNA in human trigeminal ganglia.
- [[sources/cohrs-gilden-2007-latently-transcribed-vzv-genes-human-ganglia]] supports VZV latent-transcript detection in human ganglia.
- [[sources/steain-2014-t-cell-responses-active-vzv-reactivation-human-ganglia]] supports VZV DNA and immune infiltrates in active zoster ganglia.

The current full-text set supports a meaningful latency/reactivation chain, but lesion-culture similarity, ganglionic DNA, transcripts, and active ganglionitis are different endpoints.

## Evidence ladder
| Rung | Current evidence in hand | What it can answer | What it cannot answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Varicella/zoster identity in culture | [[sources/weller-witton-bell-1958-varicella-zoster-isolation-propagation-culture]] | Varicella- and zoster-derived lesion agents were culturally similar/indistinguishable in vitro. | Ganglionic latency or reactivation trigger. |
| Human ganglia latency/reactivation | [[sources/lungu-1995-reactivated-latent-vzv-human-dorsal-root-ganglia]] | VZV material is detectable in human dorsal-root ganglia during latent/reactivated states. | Why a particular person develops shingles. |
| Neuronal localization | [[sources/kennedy-1998-latent-vzv-neurons-human-trigeminal-ganglia]] | Latent VZV DNA localizes predominantly to neurons in human trigeminal ganglia. | Future reactivation or trigger. |
| Latent transcription | [[sources/cohrs-gilden-2007-latently-transcribed-vzv-genes-human-ganglia]] | Selected VZV genes are transcribed in latently infected human ganglia. | Complete latency/reactivation mechanism. |
| Active ganglionitis | [[sources/steain-2014-t-cell-responses-active-vzv-reactivation-human-ganglia]] | Active zoster can involve VZV DNA, ganglionitis, T-cell infiltrates, and MHC changes in affected ganglia. | General frequency or causal trigger. |

## Current manuscript rule for shingles and VZV
Shingles should be presented as a latency/reactivation ladder, not merely as a reuse of chickenpox lesion-culture evidence. The manuscript may say the current full-text set supports varicella-zoster agent similarity, VZV ganglionic latency, neuronal localization, latent transcription, and active ganglionic inflammation. It should also say that those rungs do not by themselves explain why a given reactivation occurs.
