### Vaccine Clips 🔗

Vaccines demonstrably prevent specific diseases, but the claim that prevention equals health benefit requires its own evidence, which often hasn't been systematically gathered through rigorous long-term outcome studies. It's a logical inference, not a proven one. That's a legitimate methodological critique.
