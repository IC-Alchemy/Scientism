US AID

### Yearly Breakdown:

- 2021: 
  - Total Assistance: $3.8 billion 
  - Military Aid: $3.3 billion 
  - Missile Defense (Iron Dome): $500 million

- 2022: 
  - Total Assistance: Approximately $4.18 billion 
  - Military Aid: $3.18 billion 
  - Missile Defense (Iron Dome): $1 billion (appropriated for use up to FY2024)

- 2023: 
  - Total Assistance: Approximately $17.8 billion 
  - Regular Military Aid: $3.3 billion 
  - Missile Defense: $500 million 
  - Emergency Military Aid: $14.3 billion

- 2024: 
  - Total Assistance: At least $17.9 billion (excludes an additional $8 billion arms sale planned for January 2025) 
  - This includes a major package that aligns with expenditure due to ongoing conflicts.
